Ishaq ibn Mansur related to us. He said: ‘Abd al-Razzaq related to us. He said: Ma‘mar informed us, from Hammam, from Abu Hurayrah, who said: The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn al-Muthanna related to us. Yahya related to us, from Hisham, who said: My father informed me, from ‘A’ishah:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, came in to her while a woman was with her. He said: “Who is this?” She said: “So-and-so,” mentioning her prayer. He said: “Stop. Take on what you are able to bear. By Allah, Allah does not grow weary until you grow weary.” The religious practice most beloved to him was that which its practitioner continued.
