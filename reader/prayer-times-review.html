Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn Maslamah related to us. He said: I read to Malik, from Ibn Shihab:

That ‘Umar ibn ‘Abd al-‘Aziz delayed the prayer one day. ‘Urwah ibn al-Zubayr came in to him and informed him that al-Mughirah ibn Shu‘bah had delayed the prayer one day while he was in Iraq. Abu Mas‘ud al-Ansari came in to him and said: “What is this, Mughirah? Did you not know that Jibril, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, descended and prayed, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prayed? Then he prayed, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prayed. Then he prayed, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prayed. Then he prayed, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prayed. Then he prayed, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prayed. Then he said: ‘This is what I was commanded to do.’” ‘Umar said to ‘Urwah: “Know what you are relating. Was it indeed Jibril who established the time of prayer for the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace?”

‘Urwah said: That is how Bashir ibn Abi Mas‘ud used to relate it from his father. ‘Urwah said: And ‘A’ishah related to me:

That the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to pray ‘Asr while the sunlight was in her chamber, before it rose out of it.

Complete working English

Musaddad related to us. He said: Yahya related to us, from al-A‘mash, who said: Shaqiq related to me. He said: I heard Hudhayfah say:

We were sitting with ‘Umar, may Allah Most High be pleased with him. He said: “Which of you remembers the statement of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, about tribulation?” I said: “I do, as he said it.” He said: “You are certainly bold about him—or about it.” I said: “A man’s trial in his family, his wealth, his child and his neighbour is expiated by prayer, fasting, charity, commanding and forbidding.” He said: “This is not what I mean, but the tribulation that surges as the sea surges.” He said: “You have nothing to fear from it, Commander of the Believers. Between you and it is a closed door.” He said: “Will it be broken or opened?” He said: “It will be broken.” He said: “Then it will never be closed.”

We said: “Did ‘Umar know the door?” He said: “Yes, just as tonight comes before tomorrow. I related to him a report containing no misleading statements.” We were afraid to ask Hudhayfah, so we told Masruq to do so. He asked him, and he said: “The door is ‘Umar.”

Complete working English

Adam ibn Abi Iyas related to us. He said: Shu‘bah related to us. He said: Muhajir Abu al-Hasan, a freedman of Banu Taym Allah, related to us. He said: I heard Zayd ibn Wahb, from Abu Dharr al-Ghifari, who said:

We were with the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, on a journey. The caller to prayer wanted to call for Dhuhr, but the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “Let it become cooler.” Then he wanted to call, and he said to him: “Let it become cooler,” until we saw the shadows of the hillocks. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “Intense heat is from the blast of Jahannam. When the heat becomes intense, delay the prayer until it is cooler.”

And Ibn ‘Abbas said: “Yatafayya’u” means “it inclines.”
