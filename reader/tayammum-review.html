Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim, from his father, from ‘A’ishah, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, who said:

We set out with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, on one of his journeys. When we were at al-Bayda’ or Dhat al-Jaysh, a necklace of mine broke. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, stayed to search for it, and the people stayed with him. They were not at a place with water. The people came to Abu Bakr al-Siddiq and said: “Do you not see what ‘A’ishah has done? She has kept the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and the people here, where there is no water, and they have no water with them.”

Abu Bakr came while the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was asleep with his head resting on my thigh. He said: “You have detained the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and the people, where there is no water, and they have no water with them.” ‘A’ishah said: Abu Bakr reproached me, saying what Allah willed him to say, and began poking me in my side with his hand. Nothing kept me from moving except the position of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, on my thigh.

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, rose when morning came, without water. Allah then revealed the verse of tayammum, and they performed tayammum. Usayd ibn al-Hudayr said: “This is not the first of your blessings, family of Abu Bakr.” She said: We made the camel on which I had been riding stand up and found the necklace beneath it.

Muhammad ibn Sinan related to us. He said: Hushaym related to us.
