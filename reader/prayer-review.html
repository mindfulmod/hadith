Complete working English

Yahya ibn Bukayr related to us. He said: Al-Layth related to us, from Yunus, from Ibn Shihab, from Anas ibn Malik, who said: Abu Dharr used to relate that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

The roof of my house was opened while I was in Makkah. Jibril descended, opened my chest and then washed it with Zamzam water. Then he brought a basin of gold filled with wisdom and faith, poured it into my chest and closed it. Then he took my hand and ascended with me to the lowest heaven.

When I came to the lowest heaven, Jibril said to the keeper of the heaven: “Open.” He said: “Who is this?” He said: “This is Jibril.” He said: “Is anyone with you?” He said: “Yes, Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, is with me.” He said: “Has he been sent for?” He said: “Yes.”

When he opened, we ascended into the lowest heaven. There was a man sitting with figures to his right and figures to his left. When he looked towards his right, he laughed, and when he looked towards his left, he wept. He said: “Welcome to the righteous Prophet and the righteous son.” I said to Jibril: “Who is this?” He said: “This is Adam, and these figures to his right and left are the souls of his children. Those on the right are among the people of Paradise, and the figures on his left are the people of the Fire. When he looks to his right, he laughs, and when he looks towards his left, he weeps.” Then he ascended with me to the second heaven and said to its keeper: “Open.” Its keeper said to him the same as the first had said, and he opened.

Anas said: He mentioned that he found Adam, Idris, Musa, ‘Isa and Ibrahim in the heavens—may Allah’s blessings be upon them. He did not specify their positions, except that he mentioned finding Adam in the lowest heaven and Ibrahim in the sixth heaven.

Anas said: When Jibril passed by Idris with the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, he said: “Welcome to the righteous Prophet and the righteous brother.” I said: “Who is this?” He said: “This is Idris.” Then I passed by Musa, and he said: “Welcome to the righteous Prophet and the righteous brother.” I said: “Who is this?” He said: “This is Musa.” Then I passed by ‘Isa, and he said: “Welcome to the righteous brother and the righteous Prophet.” I said: “Who is this?” He said: “This is ‘Isa.” Then I passed by Ibrahim, and he said: “Welcome to the righteous Prophet and the righteous son.” I said: “Who is this?” He said: “This is Ibrahim, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.”

Ibn Shihab said: Then Ibn Hazm informed me that Ibn ‘Abbas and Abu Habbah al-Ansari used to say: The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

Then I was taken up until I reached a height where I could hear the scratching of pens.

Ibn Hazm and Anas ibn Malik said: The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

Allah made fifty prayers obligatory for my community. I returned with that until I passed by Musa, who said: “What has Allah made obligatory for you upon your community?” I said: “He has made fifty prayers obligatory.” He said: “Return to your Lord, for your community cannot bear that.” So he sent me back, and He removed part of them. I returned to Musa and said: “He has removed part of them.” He said: “Go back to your Lord, for your community cannot bear it.” I went back, and He removed part of them. I returned to him, and he said: “Return to your Lord, for your community cannot bear that.” I went back to Him, and He said: “They are five and they are fifty. The word is not changed with Me.” I returned to Musa, and he said: “Go back to your Lord.” I said: “I feel ashamed before my Lord.”

Then I was taken onwards until I reached the Lote-tree of the Utmost Boundary. Colours covered it, and I do not know what they were. Then I was brought into Paradise, and there were strings of pearls in it, and its soil was musk.
