‘Ali ibn ‘Abd Allah related to us. He said: Sufyan related to us. He said: I heard ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim. He said: I heard al-Qasim saying: I heard ‘A’ishah saying:

Complete working English

Ibrahim ibn Musa related to us. He said: Hisham ibn Yusuf informed us that Ibn Jurayj informed them. He said: Hisham informed me, from ‘Urwah, that he was asked:

“May a menstruating woman serve me, or may a woman come close to me while she is in janabah?” ‘Urwah said: “All of that is easy for me, and in all of that she serves me, and there is no harm in that for anyone.”

‘A’ishah informed me that

She used to comb—meaning the head of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace—while she was menstruating. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was at that time staying in retreat in the mosque. He would bring his head close to her while she was in her chamber, and she would comb it while she was menstruating.

Complete working English

Isma‘il ibn Khalil related to us. He said: ‘Ali ibn Mushir informed us. He said: Abu Ishaq—who is al-Shaybani—informed us, from ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Aswad, from his father, from ‘A’ishah, who said:
