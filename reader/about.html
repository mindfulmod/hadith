THE MINDFULMOD LIBRARY

About this reader.

A personal place to read and listen to Sahih al-Bukhari, in book order and at your own pace.

Your place stays on this device.

The app keeps your bookmarks, read marks, reading and listening positions, and display preferences in this browser’s local storage. It has no account, advertising, analytics or cloud sync. Exact-text searches run on your device.

Under Aa, you can download a backup or restore one. Restore reads the file locally; the app does not upload it. A backup is a readable JSON file, so anyone you share it with can see the reading information inside. Audio downloads are separate.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening it or downloading a file makes requests to GitHub, which handles hosting information under its privacy statement ↗. Opening an external source link visits that source’s website.

Taking a session offline.

On your session page, use Save reading to keep all available hadith, source notes and artwork. Save audio separately for the Cedar recording or optional review clips; saving audio also saves the reading material. Wait for each chosen download to say Ready offline. External source websites and publisher PDFs still need a connection.

Your browser or phone may remove stored downloads when space is needed. Use Check downloads before travelling, and keep a separate backup of your bookmarks. Interrupted saves reuse finished, checked files. You can remove audio or older downloads without removing your bookmarks. Pause a recording before removing its download. Safari and an app added to the Home Screen may have separate saved copies.

Text and listening status.

This personal prototype contains the first seven hadith. Its English is an AI-assisted working translation, compared against Arabic witnesses and two named translators. It is not the Darussalam translation and is not independently certified. Full narrator introductions are expandable; letters and transmission notes remain in source order.

Cedar is an AI-generated voice, currently available for Hadith 1–2. You can read and listen together, but the generated speech still needs an exact-word and pronunciation review. Playback tests and file-integrity checks do not establish translation or spoken accuracy.

Arabic & sources beside each hadith provides the Arabic text, edition references, comparison links and attribution. The Hadith 1–2 comparison, Hadith 3–7 wording choices and narration review remain accessible.

Attribution.

Sahih al-Bukhari by Muhammad ibn Ismail al-Bukhari. The selected Arabic digital text is from Arabic Wikisource contributors, revision 484874. Hadith 1–2 are unchanged. Hadith 3–7 use a separately versioned personal edition, with one declared Arabic correction in Hadith 5 supported by two printed witnesses. The original inputs are preserved. The new English translation and comparison notes were produced with AI assistance.

Report a text concern.

Please include the hadith number, the exact wording in question, and a source or printed page we can compare. We record differences rather than silently combining editions.

Open a source correction report ↗

GitHub reports are public. Keep personal backup files and private reading information out of the report.

Keeping the reader current.

Updates wait for open reader pages to close so they do not replace an active reading or listening session. Check for app updates when you are ready.
