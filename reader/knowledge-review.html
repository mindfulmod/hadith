Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Al-Layth related to us, from Sa‘id—he is al-Maqburi—from Sharik ibn ‘Abdullah ibn Abi Namir, that he heard Anas ibn Malik saying:

While we were sitting with the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, in the mosque, a man entered on a camel. He made it kneel in the mosque and then hobbled it. We said: “This fair-skinned man who is reclining.” The man said to him: “Son of ‘Abd al-Muttalib!” The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said to him: “I have answered you.” The man said to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace: “I am going to question you and be insistent in questioning you, so do not feel anger toward me.” He said: “Ask whatever occurs to you.” He said: “I ask you by your Lord and the Lord of those before you: did Allah send you to all people?” He said: “O Allah, yes.” He said: “I ask you by Allah: did Allah command you that we should perform the five prayers in the day and night?” He said: “O Allah, yes.” He said: “I ask you by Allah: did Allah command you that we should fast this month of the year?” He said: “O Allah, yes.” He said: “I ask you by Allah: did Allah command you to take this charity from our rich and distribute it among our poor?” The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “O Allah, yes.” The man said: “I believe in what you have brought. I am a messenger from those of my people whom I have left behind. I am Dimam ibn Tha‘labah, a member of Banu Sa‘d ibn Bakr.”

Musa and ‘Ali ibn ‘Abd al-Hamid transmitted this report from Sulayman, from Thabit, from Anas, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.
