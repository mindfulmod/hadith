Complete working English

‘Abdullah related to us; he heard Yazid, who said: Humayd informed us, from Anas, who said:

One night the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, delayed the prayer until half the night had passed. Then he came out to us. When he had prayed, he turned his face towards us and said, “The people have prayed and gone to sleep, but you will remain in prayer as long as you wait for the prayer.”

Hadith 812

AI-assisted working translation. Independent review pending.

Open this hadith →

Comparison findings

  • All eight named routes, both accounts, five prophetic honorifics, the written transmission and the marriage/alliance detail remain. The informed/related distinctions and Firasiyyah/Qurashiyyah identifications are not collapsed. The historical print calls Hind Qurashiyyah in Uthman’s route, where the selected text and modern print say Firasiyyah.
  • We think stays plural, unlike the I think of 802. The modern print separates labels 849 and 850; the selected complete report retains both. The Khan-family comparison omits the full closing variants, and Bewley condenses them into a general variations note; neither is used to abridge the Arabic.

AI-assisted translation of every selected Arabic span, including full chains, repeated actions, uncertainty and narrator exchanges. Two visually inspected Arabic prints and individually mapped English comparisons were consulted. Independent Arabic, English, chapter, reference and spoken-word review remains pending.

Complete working English

Abu al-Walid related to us. Ibrahim ibn Sa‘d related to us. Al-Zuhri related to us, from Hind bint al-Harith, from Umm Salamah:

When the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said the closing greeting, he would remain in his place a little while.

Ibn Shihab said: We think—and Allah knows best—that this was so those of the women who were leaving could depart.

Ibn Abi Maryam said: Nafi‘ ibn Yazid informed us. He said: Ja‘far ibn Rabi‘ah informed me that Ibn Shihab wrote to him, saying: Hind bint al-Harith al-Firasiyyah related to me, from Umm Salamah, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace—and she was one of her companions—who said:

He would say the closing greeting, and the women would leave and enter their homes before the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, departed.

Ibn Wahb said, from Yunus, from Ibn Shihab: Hind al-Firasiyyah informed me. ‘Uthman ibn ‘Umar said: Yunus informed us, from al-Zuhri: Hind al-Firasiyyah related to me. Al-Zubaydi said: Al-Zuhri informed me that Hind bint al-Harith al-Qurashiyyah informed him. She was married to Ma‘bad ibn al-Miqdad, who was an ally of Banu Zuhrah, and she used to visit the wives of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. Shu‘ayb said, from al-Zuhri: Hind al-Qurashiyyah related to me. Ibn Abi ‘Atiq said, from al-Zuhri, from Hind al-Firasiyyah. Al-Layth said: Yahya ibn Sa‘id related to me; he related to him from Ibn Shihab, from a woman of Quraysh who related to him from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn ‘Ubayd related to us. He said: ‘Isa ibn Yunus related to us, from ‘Umar ibn Sa‘id, who said: Ibn Abi Mulaykah informed me, from ‘Uqbah, who said:

I prayed the afternoon prayer behind the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, in Madinah. He said the closing greeting, then rose quickly and stepped over the people’s necks towards one of his wives’ rooms. The people were startled by his haste. He came out to them and saw that they were surprised by his haste, so he said, “I remembered some uncoined gold we had. I disliked its holding me back, so I ordered it to be distributed.”

Sa‘id ibn ‘Ufayr related to us. He said: Ibn Wahb related to us, from Yunus, from Ibn Shihab: ‘Ata’ stated that Jabir ibn ‘Abdullah stated that the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

“Whoever eats garlic or onions should keep away from us”; or he said, “Should keep away from our mosque and stay in his house.” The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was brought a pot containing green vegetables. He noticed a smell from them, asked about it and was told what vegetables it contained. He said, “Bring it near,” to one of his companions who was with him. When he saw it, he disliked eating it. He said, “Eat, for I converse privately with those with whom you do not converse.”

Ahmad ibn Salih said, from Ibn Wahb: He was brought a badr. Ibn Wahb said: Meaning a platter containing greens. Al-Layth and Abu Safwan did not mention the story of the pot from Yunus. I do not know whether it is a statement of al-Zuhri or part of the hadith. Ahmad ibn Salih said, after Yunus’s report from Ibn Shihab: He confirms Yunus’s statement.
