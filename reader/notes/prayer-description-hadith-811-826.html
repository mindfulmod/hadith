Complete working English

Abu al-Walid related to us. Ibrahim ibn Sa‘d related to us. Al-Zuhri related to us, from Hind bint al-Harith, from Umm Salamah:

When the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said the closing greeting, he would remain in his place a little while.

Ibn Shihab said: We think—and Allah knows best—that this was so those of the women who were leaving could depart.

Ibn Abi Maryam said: Nafi‘ ibn Yazid informed us. He said: Ja‘far ibn Rabi‘ah informed me that Ibn Shihab wrote to him, saying: Hind bint al-Harith al-Firasiyyah related to me, from Umm Salamah, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace—and she was one of her companions—who said:

He would say the closing greeting, and the women would leave and enter their homes before the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, departed.

Ibn Wahb said, from Yunus, from Ibn Shihab: Hind al-Firasiyyah informed me. ‘Uthman ibn ‘Umar said: Yunus informed us, from al-Zuhri: Hind al-Firasiyyah related to me. Al-Zubaydi said: Al-Zuhri informed me that Hind bint al-Harith al-Qurashiyyah informed him. She was married to Ma‘bad ibn al-Miqdad, who was an ally of Banu Zuhrah, and she used to visit the wives of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. Shu‘ayb said, from al-Zuhri: Hind al-Qurashiyyah related to me. Ibn Abi ‘Atiq said, from al-Zuhri, from Hind al-Firasiyyah. Al-Layth said: Yahya ibn Sa‘id related to me; he related to him from Ibn Shihab, from a woman of Quraysh who related to him from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.
