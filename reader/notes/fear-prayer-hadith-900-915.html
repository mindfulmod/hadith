Comparison findings

Complete working English

Abu al-Yaman related to us. He said: Shu‘ayb informed us, from al-Zuhri.

He said: I asked him, ‘Did the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, pray?’—meaning the fear prayer. He said: Salim informed me that ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Umar, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, said:

I went on an expedition with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, towards Najd. We faced the enemy and formed ranks opposite them. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, stood to lead us in prayer. One group stood with him, praying, while another group faced the enemy. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, bowed with those who were with him and made two prostrations. Then they went to take the place of the group that had not prayed. That group came, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prayed one unit with them and made two prostrations. Then he gave the closing greeting. Each of them then stood and prayed one unit for himself and made two prostrations.

Sa‘id ibn Yahya ibn Sa‘id al-Qurashi related to us. He said: My father related to me. He said: Ibn Jurayj related to us, from Musa ibn ‘Uqbah, from Nafi‘, from Ibn ‘Umar:

Complete working English

Haywah ibn Shurayh related to us. He said: Muhammad ibn Harb related to us, from al-Zubaydi, from al-Zuhri, from ‘Ubaydullah ibn ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Utbah, from Ibn ‘Abbas, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both. He said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, stood, and the people stood with him. He said ‘Allah is greatest,’ and they said it with him. He bowed, and some of the people bowed. Then he prostrated, and they prostrated with him. Then he stood for the second unit. Those who had prostrated stood and guarded their brothers. The other group came and bowed and prostrated with him. All the people were in prayer, but they were guarding one another.

Comparison findings

Complete working English

Yahya related to us. He said: Waki‘ related to us, from ‘Ali ibn Mubarak, from Yahya ibn Abi Kathir, from Abu Salamah, from Jabir ibn ‘Abdullah. He said:

‘Umar came on the day of the Ditch and began cursing the unbelievers of Quraysh, saying, ‘Messenger of Allah, I had not prayed ‘Asr until the sun was almost setting.’ The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, ‘And by Allah, I have not prayed it yet either.’ He said: He went down to Buthan, performed ablution and prayed ‘Asr after the sun had set. Then he prayed Maghrib after it.

Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Muhammad ibn Asma’ related to us. He said: Juwayriyah related to us, from Nafi‘, from Ibn ‘Umar. He said:

When the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, returned from al-Ahzab, he said to us, ‘Let no one pray ‘Asr except at Banu Qurayzah.’ The time for ‘Asr reached some of them on the way. Complete working English

Musaddad related to us. He said: Hammad related to us, from ‘Abd al-‘Aziz ibn Suhayb and Thabit al-Bunani, from Anas ibn Malik:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prayed the dawn prayer while it was still dark. Then he mounted and said, ‘Allah is greatest! Khaybar is ruined. When we descend into a people’s open space, evil is the morning of those who have been warned.’ They came out running in the streets, saying, ‘Muhammad and al-khamis!’ He said: Al-khamis means the army. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, overcame them, killed the fighting men and took the children captive. Safiyyah came to Dihyah al-Kalbi, and then to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. He then married her and made her freedom her bridal gift.

‘Abd al-‘Aziz said to Thabit, ‘Abu Muhammad, did you ask Anas what he gave her as a bridal gift?’ He said, ‘He gave her herself as her bridal gift.’ Then he smiled.
