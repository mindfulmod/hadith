Complete working English

Al-Hakam ibn Nafi‘ related to us. He said: Shu‘ayb informed us, from al-Zuhri, who said: ‘Amir ibn Sa‘d related to me, from Sa‘d ibn Abi Waqqas, who informed him that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

“Indeed, you will be rewarded for any spending by which you seek Allah’s Face, even what you put into your wife’s mouth.”

Comparison findings

  • The date context, praise of Allah, call for dignity and calm, request for pardon, pledge condition, oath and closing descent are retained. This is Jarir’s address with an embedded report of his pledge; the entire speech is not attributed directly to the Prophet.
  • The selected source asks pardon for your leader without an inserted late qualifier. The Prophet’s condition retains the conjunction And sincerity toward every Muslim. No unprinted pledge conditions are supplied.
  • This is the final numbered report of the pinned Book of Faith source. Completing its source coverage does not certify the English, the chapter introductions or the full Bukhari collection.

Complete working English

Abu al-Nu‘man related to us. He said: Abu ‘Awanah related to us, from Ziyad ibn ‘Ilaqah, who said:

I heard Jarir ibn ‘Abdullah speak on the day al-Mughirah ibn Shu‘bah died. He stood, praised Allah and extolled Him, and said: “You must fear Allah alone, who has no partner, and maintain dignity and calm until a leader comes to you, for he will come to you shortly.” Then he said: “Ask pardon for your leader, for he loved pardon.”

Then he said: “Now then, I came to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and said: ‘I pledge allegiance to you upon Islam.’ He stipulated for me: ‘And sincerity toward every Muslim.’ So I pledged allegiance to him on this. By the Lord of this mosque, I am sincere toward you.” Then he sought forgiveness and came down.
