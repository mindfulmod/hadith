Complete working English

Abu al-Qasim Khalid ibn Khali related to us. He said: Muhammad ibn Harb related to us. He said: Al-Awza‘i said: Al-Zuhri informed us, from ‘Ubaydullah ibn ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Utbah ibn Mas‘ud, from Ibn ‘Abbas:

I heard the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, mention his story, saying:”

“While Musa was among a gathering of the Children of Israel, a man came and asked: “Do you know anyone more knowledgeable than you?” Musa said: “No.” Allah, mighty and majestic, revealed to Musa: “Indeed, Our servant Khidr.” He asked how to meet him. Allah made the fish a sign for him, and he was told: “When you lose the fish, go back, for you will meet him.” Musa, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was following the fish’s trail in the sea. Musa’s young companion said to Musa: {Did you see? When we took shelter at the rock, I forgot the fish; none but Satan made me forget to mention it.} Musa said: {That is what we were seeking. So they retraced their steps.} They found Khidr, and what happened between them was what Allah recounted in His Book.”

Comparison findings

  • All three kinds of ground, the people’s drinking, watering and cultivation, and both concluding comparisons are retained. The two English report-body comparisons are abbreviated in their treatment of the final transmission note: Khan-family omits it; Bewley gives only the ground definitions. They do not verify its full wording.
  • The selected closing note and modern print read قيّلت; the historical print reads قبلت and omits both the Abu ‘Abdullah introduction and the qa‘/al-safsaf definitions. “Drank in” is a provisional rendering of the selected قيّلت, not a harmonization to قبلت.

The provisional rendering follows the drinking explanation; this does not settle the variant. This commentary is lexical evidence only and is not inserted into the hadith. The complete closing source note remains distinct from the Prophet’s quoted words.

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn al-‘Ala’ related to us. He said: Hammad ibn Usamah related to us, from Burayd ibn ‘Abdullah, from Abu Burdah, from Abu Musa, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, who said:

“The guidance and knowledge with which Allah sent me are like abundant rain falling on land. Some of it was good ground that accepted the water and brought forth plentiful pasture and fresh grass. Some of it was hard ground that held the water, through which Allah benefited people: they drank, watered and cultivated. It fell on another tract that was only level ground, neither holding water nor growing pasture. This is the likeness of one who gains understanding of Allah’s religion and benefits from what Allah sent me with, learning and teaching; and the likeness of one who pays it no heed and does not accept Allah’s guidance with which I was sent.”

Abu ‘Abdullah said: Ishaq said: “And among it was a tract that drank in the water.” A qa‘ is ground over which water rises, and al-safsaf is level ground.

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Kathir related to us. He said: Sufyan informed us, from Ibn Abi Khalid, from Qays ibn Abi Hazim, from Abu Mas‘ud al-Ansari, who said:

A man said: “Messenger of Allah, I can hardly attend the prayer because so-and-so prolongs it for us.” I never saw the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, more angry while admonishing than on that day. He said: “People, you drive others away. Whoever leads people in prayer should make it light, for among them are the sick, the weak and those with a need.”
