Muhammad related to us. He said: Abu Damrah Anas ibn ‘Iyad informed us. He said: Sharik ibn ‘Abdullah ibn Abi Namir related to us that he heard Anas ibn Malik mentioning:

A man entered on Friday through a door facing the pulpit while the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was standing and delivering the sermon. He stood facing the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and said, “O Messenger of Allah, the livestock have perished and the roads have been cut off, so supplicate to Allah to send us rain.” He said: The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, raised his hands and said, “O Allah, give us rain. Then a man entered through that door on the following Friday while the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was standing and delivering the sermon. He stood facing him and said, “O Messenger of Allah, property has been destroyed and the roads have been cut off, so supplicate to Allah to hold it back.” He said: The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, raised his hands, then said, “O Allah, around us and not upon us. O Allah, upon the high ground, the mountains, the thickets, the low hills, the valleys and the places where trees grow.” He said: It stopped, and we went out walking in the sunshine.
