Complete working English

Qutaybah related to us. Mughirah ibn ‘Abd al-Rahman related to us, from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A‘raj, from Abu Hurayrah:

When the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, raised his head in the last unit of prayer, he would say, “O Allah, rescue ‘Ayyash ibn Abi Rabi‘ah. O Allah, rescue Salamah ibn Hisham. O Allah, rescue al-Walid ibn al-Walid. O Allah, rescue the oppressed believers. O Allah, intensify Your pressure upon Mudar. O Allah, make it years like the years of Yusuf.” And the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, “Ghifar—may Allah forgive it; and Aslam—may Allah grant it peace.”

Ibn Abi al-Zinad said, from his father: All of this was in the morning prayer.

Complete working English

‘Uthman ibn Abi Shaybah related to us. He said: Jarir related to us, from Mansur, from Abu al-Duha, from Masruq, who said:

We were with ‘Abdullah, and he said: When the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, saw the people turning away, he said, “O Allah, seven like the seven of Yusuf.” A year of scarcity overtook them and consumed everything, until they ate hides, carrion and decaying carcasses. One of them would look at the sky and see smoke from hunger. Abu Sufyan came to him and said, “O Muhammad, you command obedience to Allah and maintaining ties of kinship. Your people have perished, so supplicate to Allah for them.” Allah Most High said, “So watch for the day when the sky brings clear smoke,” up to His words, “returning—the day We strike the greatest blow.” The blow was on the day of Badr. The smoke, the blow, al-lizam and the sign of the Romans have already passed.

Complete working English

‘Amr ibn ‘Ali related to us. He said: Abu Qutaybah related to us. He said: ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn ‘Abdullah ibn Dinar related to us, from his father, who said:

I heard Ibn ‘Umar reciting Abu Talib’s poetry:

And a fair one, through whose face rain is sought from the clouds,

a refuge for orphans, a protection for widows.

And ‘Umar ibn Hamzah said: Salim related to us, from his father:

At times I remembered the poet’s words while looking at the face of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, as he sought rain. He would not come down until every waterspout gushed:

And a fair one, through whose face rain is sought from the clouds,

a refuge for orphans, a protection for widows.

And these are Abu Talib’s words.
