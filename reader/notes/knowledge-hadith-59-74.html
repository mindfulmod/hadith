Hadith 59

AI-assisted working translation. Independent review pending.

Comparison findings

  • Both chain routes and the change-of-chain marker remain. The selected Arabic and modern print have Muhammad ibn Fulayh say “my father related to me”; the historical print has “related to us”. The selected singular is retained.
  • The selected digital phrase أين أراه السائل contains أراه between “where” and “the questioner”. Both printed witnesses omit this word here, as do the two English comparisons. The provisional “I think” preserves it as an interruption without assigning the uncertainty to a particular narrator. Its reading and speaker remain unresolved; the Arabic is not silently corrected.
  • The two differing audience reactions, the first reply about the trust, the follow-up question and the final condition all remain. “Authority” is provisional for الأمر in this context; no modern political application is added.

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Sinan related to us. He said: Fulayh related to us.

Another chain: And Ibrahim ibn al-Mundhir related to me. He said: Muhammad ibn Fulayh related to us. He said: My father related to me. He said: Hilal ibn ‘Ali related to me, from ‘Ata’ ibn Yasar, from Abu Hurayrah, who said:

While the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was in a gathering speaking to the people, a Bedouin came to him and said: “When is the Hour?” The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, continued speaking. Some of the people said: “He heard what he said and disliked what he said.” Others said: “Rather, he did not hear.” When he finished speaking, he said: “Where—I think—is the one who asked about the Hour?” He said: “Here I am, Messenger of Allah.” He said: “When the trust is lost, await the Hour.” He said: “How is it lost?” He said: “When authority is entrusted to those unfit for it, await the Hour.”

Comparison findings

  • The complete questions, all four اللهم نعم answers, the rich-to-poor direction of the charity, the messenger’s declaration and his tribal affiliation remain. “O Allah, yes” retains the repeated invocation; “fair-skinned” provisionally renders الأبيض as a physical description without importing a modern racial identity.
  • The closing Musa and ‘Ali ibn ‘Abd al-Hamid/Sulayman/Thabit/Anas transmission note is retained. The named English comparisons abbreviate or omit that route; they do not independently verify the complete English chain.
  • The historical print says “al-Layth related to me”; the selected source and modern print say “related to us”. The historical witness also has a further full report after this closing route, on PDF pages 26–27, which is absent as a separate numbered report in the selected digital sequence. No extra entry is invented or spliced into 63.
  • The selected source has separate chapters 6 and 7. The modern print groups the knowledge verse and reading-to-the-transmitter material under chapter 6; the historical print’s chapter 6 begins with the reading heading. The reader preserves the selected source’s grouping.

Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Al-Layth related to us, from Sa‘id—he is al-Maqburi—from Sharik ibn ‘Abdullah ibn Abi Namir, that he heard Anas ibn Malik saying:

While we were sitting with the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, in the mosque, a man entered on a camel. He made it kneel in the mosque and then hobbled it. Then he said to them: “Which of you is Muhammad?” The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was reclining among them. We said: “This fair-skinned man who is reclining.” The man said to him: “Son of ‘Abd al-Muttalib!” The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said to him: “I have answered you.” The man said to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace: “I am going to question you and be insistent in questioning you, so do not feel anger toward me.” He said: “Ask whatever occurs to you.” He said: “I ask you by your Lord and the Lord of those before you: did Allah send you to all people?” He said: “O Allah, yes.” He said: “I ask you by Allah: did Allah command you that we should perform the five prayers in the day and night?” He said: “O Allah, yes.” He said: “I ask you by Allah: did Allah command you that we should fast this month of the year?” He said: “O Allah, yes.” He said: “I ask you by Allah: did Allah command you to take this charity from our rich and distribute it among our poor?” The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “O Allah, yes.” The man said: “I believe in what you have brought. I am a messenger from those of my people whom I have left behind. I am Dimam ibn Tha‘labah, a member of Banu Sa‘d ibn Bakr.”

Musa and ‘Ali ibn ‘Abd al-Hamid transmitted this report from Sulayman, from Thabit, from Anas, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Ghurayr al-Zuhri related to me. He said: Ya‘qub ibn Ibrahim related to us. He said: My father related to me, from Salih, from Ibn Shihab, who related that ‘Ubaydullah ibn ‘Abdullah informed him, from Ibn ‘Abbas:

He and al-Hurr ibn Qays ibn Hisn al-Fazari disputed about Musa’s companion. Ibn ‘Abbas said: “He was Khidr.” Ubayy ibn Ka‘b passed by them, and Ibn ‘Abbas called him and said: “This companion of mine and I have disputed about Musa’s companion, whom Musa asked how to meet. Have you heard the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, mention his story?” He said: “Yes. I heard the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, say:”

“While Musa was among a gathering of the Children of Israel, a man came and asked: “Do you know anyone more knowledgeable than you?” Musa said: “No.” Allah revealed to Musa: “Indeed, Our servant Khidr.” Musa asked how to reach him. Allah made the fish a sign for him, and he was told: “When you lose the fish, go back, for you will meet him.” He was following the fish’s trail in the sea. Musa’s young companion said to him: {Did you see? When we took shelter at the rock, I forgot the fish; none but Satan made me forget to mention it.} {He said: That is what we were seeking. So they retraced their steps.} They found Khidr, and what happened between them was what Allah, mighty and majestic, recounted in His Book.”
