Complete working English

Musaddad related to us. He said: Yahya ibn Sa‘id related to us, from ‘Ubaydullah. He said: Nafi‘ informed us, from Ibn ‘Umar, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, who said:

I prayed with the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, two rak‘ahs before Dhuhr, two rak‘ahs after Dhuhr, two rak‘ahs after Maghrib, two rak‘ahs after ‘Isha’, and two rak‘ahs after Jumu‘ah. Ishaq related to me. Ya‘qub ibn Ibrahim related to us. My father related to us, from Ibn Shihab, who said: Mahmud ibn al-Rabi‘ al-Ansari informed me:

He remembered the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and remembered a mouthful of water that the Messenger sprayed into his face from a well that was in their house.

Mahmud related that he heard ‘Itban ibn Malik al-Ansari, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, who was among those who witnessed Badr with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, say: “I used to lead my people in prayer among Banu Salim. A valley lay between me and them; when the rains came, it was difficult for me to cross it toward their mosque. I came to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and said to him, ‘I have found my eyesight impaired, and the valley between me and my people flows when the rains come, making it difficult for me to cross it. I would like you to come and pray in a place in my house that I could take as a prayer place.’ The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, ‘I will do so.’”

“The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and Abu Bakr, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, came to me in the morning after the day had advanced. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, asked permission to enter, and I gave him permission. He did not sit until he said, ‘Where in your house would you like me to pray?’ I pointed out to him the place where I liked to pray. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, stood, said the takbir, and we formed rows behind him. He prayed two rak‘ahs, then said the taslim, and we said the taslim when he did.”

“I kept him for khazir that was being prepared for him. The people of the neighbourhood heard that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was in my house, and men from among them gathered until there were many men in the house. One of them said, ‘What has happened to Malik? I do not see him.’ One of them said, ‘He is a hypocrite who does not love Allah and His Messenger.’”

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, “Do not say that. Do you not see that he has said, ‘There is no god except Allah,’ seeking by that the face of Allah?” He said, “Allah and His Messenger know best. As for us, by Allah, we see his affection and his conversation directed only toward the hypocrites.” The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, “For Allah has forbidden to the Fire whoever says, ‘There is no god except Allah,’ seeking by that the face of Allah.”

Mahmud said: I related it to some people among whom was Abu Ayyub, the companion of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, during his expedition in which he died, with Yazid ibn Mu‘awiyah over them in the land of the Romans. Abu Ayyub objected to it, saying to me, “By Allah, I do not think the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, ever said what you have said.” That weighed heavily on me, so I made a vow to Allah that, if He kept me safe until I returned from my expedition, I would ask ‘Itban ibn Malik, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, about it if I found him alive in the mosque of his people.

I returned and entered ihram for a Hajj or an ‘Umrah. Then I travelled until I reached Medina and came to Banu Salim. There was ‘Itban, an old blind man, leading his people in prayer. When he said the taslim from the prayer, I greeted him, told him who I was, and then asked him about that report. He related it to me as he had related it to me the first time.
