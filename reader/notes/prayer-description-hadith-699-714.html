Complete working English

Abu al-Yaman related to us. He said: Shu‘ayb informed us, from al-Zuhri, who said: Anas ibn Malik al-Ansari informed me:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, rode a horse and his right side was grazed. Anas, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, said: That day he led us in one of the prayers while seated, and we prayed seated behind him. Then, after he gave the greeting of peace, he said, “The imam has only been appointed to be followed. When he prays standing, pray standing. When he bows, bow; when he rises, rise; and when he prostrates, prostrate. When he says, ‘Allah hears whoever praises Him,’ say, ‘Our Lord, and to You belongs praise.’”

Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Maslamah related to us, from Malik, from Ibn Shihab, from Salim ibn ‘Abdullah, from his father:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to raise his hands level with his shoulders when he began the prayer and when he said the takbir for bowing. When he raised his head from bowing, he also raised them in the same way and said, “Allah hears whoever praises Him. Our Lord, and to You belongs praise.” He did not do that in prostration.

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Muqatil related to us. He said: ‘Abdullah informed us. He said: Yunus informed us, from al-Zuhri: Salim ibn ‘Abdullah informed me, from ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Umar, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, who said:

I saw the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, raise his hands until they were level with his shoulders when he stood in prayer. He would do that when he said the takbir for bowing, and would do that when he raised his head from bowing, saying, “Allah hears whoever praises Him.” He would not do that in prostration.
