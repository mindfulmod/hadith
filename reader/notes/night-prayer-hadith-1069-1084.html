Complete working English

‘Ali ibn ‘Abdullah related to us. He said: Sufyan related to us. He said: Sulayman ibn Abi Muslim related to us, from Tawus, who heard Ibn ‘Abbas, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, say:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, when he rose during the night for tahajjud, would say: “O Allah, to You belongs praise. You are the Sustainer of the heavens and the earth and those within them. To You belongs praise. Yours is the dominion of the heavens and the earth and those within them. To You belongs praise. You are the Light of the heavens and the earth and those within them. To You belongs praise. You are the King of the heavens and the earth. To You belongs praise. You are the Truth. Your promise is the truth; meeting You is true; Your word is true; Paradise is true; the Fire is true; the prophets are true; Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, is true; and the Hour is true. O Allah, to You I have submitted, in You I have believed, on You I have relied, and to You I have turned. By You I have argued, and to You I have brought my case for judgment. Forgive me what I have done earlier and later, what I have concealed and what I have made public. You are the One who brings forward and the One who puts back. There is no god except You,” or, “There is no god other than You.”

Sufyan said: ‘Abd al-Karim Abu Umayyah added, “And there is no power and no strength except through Allah.”

Sufyan said: Sulayman ibn Abi Muslim said that he heard it from Tawus, from Ibn ‘Abbas, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.

Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Muhammad related to us. He said: Hisham related to us. He said: Ma‘mar informed us. And Mahmud related to me. He said: ‘Abd al-Razzaq related to us. He said: Ma‘mar informed us, from al-Zuhri, from Salim, from his father, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, who said:

During the lifetime of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, when a man saw a dream, he would recount it to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. I wished to see a dream so that I could recount it to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. I was a young man, and I used to sleep in the mosque during the time of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. In my sleep I saw as though two angels took me and brought me to the Fire. It was built around like the lining of a well, and it had two uprights. There were people in it whom I recognized. I began saying, “I seek refuge in Allah from the Fire.” He said: Then another angel met us and said to me, “You need not be afraid.”

I recounted it to Hafsah, and Hafsah recounted it to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. He said, “What a fine man ‘Abdullah is! If only he prayed during the night.” After that, he slept only a little during the night.
