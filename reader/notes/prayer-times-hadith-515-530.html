Complete working English

Abu al-Yaman related to us. He said: Shu‘ayb informed us, from al-Zuhri, who said: Anas ibn Malik informed me:

That the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, came out when the sun had begun to decline and prayed Dhuhr. He stood on the pulpit and mentioned the Hour, saying that it would contain momentous events. Then he said: “Whoever wishes to ask about something, let him ask. You will not ask me about anything without my telling you, as long as I remain in this place.” The people wept a great deal, and he repeatedly said: “Ask me.” ‘Abd Allah ibn Hudhafah al-Sahmi stood up and said: “Who is my father?” He said: “Your father is Hudhafah.” Then he repeatedly said: “Ask me.” ‘Umar knelt on his knees and said: “We are content with Allah as Lord, Islam as religion, and Muhammad as Prophet.” He fell silent, then said: “The Garden and the Fire were shown to me just now across this wall. I have never seen the like of that good and evil.”

Complete working English

Hafs ibn ‘Umar related to us. He said: Shu‘bah related to us, from Abu al-Minhal, from Abu Barzah:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to pray Subh when one of us could recognize the person sitting beside him, and he would recite between sixty and a hundred in it. He prayed Dhuhr when the sun had declined, and ‘Asr when one of us could go to the farthest part of Madinah and return while the sun was still bright.

And I forgot what he said about Maghrib.

And we did not mind delaying ‘Isha’ until a third of the night. Then he said: “Until half of the night.”

And Mu‘adh said: Shu‘bah said: Then I met him on one occasion, and he said: “Or a third of the night.”

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Muqatil related to us. He said: ‘Abd Allah informed us. He said: ‘Awf informed us, from Sayyar ibn Salamah, who said:

My father and I went in to Abu Barzah al-Aslami.
