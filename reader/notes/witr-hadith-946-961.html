Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from Nafi‘ and ‘Abdullah ibn Dinar, from Ibn ‘Umar:

A man asked the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, about the night prayer. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said, “The night prayer is two units at a time. If one of you fears the coming of morning, he prays one unit, making what he has prayed odd in number.”

And from Nafi‘:

‘Abdullah ibn ‘Umar used to give the closing greeting between the single unit and the two units of Witr, even giving instructions about some need of his.

Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Maslamah related to us, from Malik, from Makhramah ibn Sulayman, from Kurayb, that Ibn ‘Abbas informed him:

He spent the night with Maymunah, who was his maternal aunt. I lay across the width of a pillow, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and his wife lay along its length. He slept until midnight, or close to it. Then he awoke, wiping sleep from his face, and recited ten verses from Al ‘Imran. Then the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, went to an old waterskin that was hanging and performed ablution well. Then he stood to pray. I did as he had done and stood beside him. He placed his right hand on my head and took hold of my ear, twisting it. Then he prayed two units, then two units, then two units, then two units, then two units, then two units. Then he prayed Witr. Then he lay down until the caller to prayer came to him. He got up and prayed two units, then went out and prayed the morning prayer.

Yahya ibn Sulayman related to us. He said: Ibn Wahb related to me. He said: ‘Amr informed me that ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim related to him, from his father, from ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Umar. He said:

Complete working English

Abu al-Yaman related to us. He said: Shu‘ayb informed us, from al-Zuhri, from ‘Urwah, that ‘A’ishah informed him:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to pray eleven units. That was his prayer.
