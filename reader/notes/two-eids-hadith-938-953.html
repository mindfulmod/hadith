Complete working English

Musaddad related to us. He said: Abu al-Ahwas related to us. He said: Mansur ibn al-Mu‘tamir related to us, from al-Sha‘bi, from al-Bara’ ibn ‘Azib. He said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, addressed us on the Day of Sacrifice after the prayer. He said, ‘Whoever prays our prayer and performs our sacrifice has fulfilled the sacrifice. Whoever sacrifices before the prayer, that is a sheep for meat.’ Abu Burdah ibn Niyar stood and said, ‘Messenger of Allah, by Allah, I sacrificed before going out to the prayer. I knew that today was a day of eating and drinking, so I made haste, ate and fed my family and my neighbours.’ The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, ‘That is a sheep for meat.’ He said, ‘I have a young she-goat that is better than two sheep for meat. Will it suffice for me?’ He said, ‘Yes, but it will not suffice for anyone after you.’

Complete working English

Hamid ibn ‘Umar related to us, from Hammad ibn Zayd, from Ayyub, from Muhammad, that Anas ibn Malik said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prayed on the Day of Sacrifice, then delivered a sermon. He commanded whoever had slaughtered before the prayer to repeat his slaughter. Complete working English

Yahya ibn Bukayr related to us. He said: Al-Layth related to us, from ‘Uqayl, from Ibn Shihab, from ‘Urwah, from ‘A’ishah:

Abu Bakr, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, came in to her. There were two girls with her during the days of Mina, playing the duff and beating it, while the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was covered with his garment. Abu Bakr rebuked them, so the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, uncovered his face and said, ‘Leave them, Abu Bakr, for these are days of Eid.’

Those days were the days of Mina.

‘A’ishah said: I saw the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, screening me while I watched the Abyssinians playing in the mosque. ‘Umar rebuked them, so the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, ‘Leave them. You are safe, Banu Arfidah.’

Meaning, in the sense of safety.
