Comparison findings

  • The first-person I heard and every chain link remain. Abu Burdah ibn Niyar is named as a man of the Ansar, without borrowing the maternal-uncle description from another occurrence.

Complete working English

Adam related to us. He said: Shu‘bah related to us. He said: Zubayd related to us. He said: I heard al-Sha‘bi, from al-Bara’ ibn ‘Azib. He said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, ‘The first thing we begin with on this day of ours is to pray, then return and sacrifice. Whoever does that has followed our practice. Whoever sacrifices before the prayer has merely presented meat to his family; it is no part of the sacrifice.’ A man of the Ansar called Abu Burdah ibn Niyar said, ‘Messenger of Allah, I have slaughtered, and I have a young she-goat better than a mature animal.’ He said, ‘Put it in its place, but it will not fulfil—or suffice—for anyone after you.’

Complete working English

Zakariyya ibn Yahya, Abu al-Sukayn, related to us. He said: Al-Muharibi related to us. He said: Muhammad ibn Suqah related to us, from Sa‘id ibn Jubayr. He said:

I was with Ibn ‘Umar when the point of a spear struck the arch of his foot, and his foot stuck to the stirrup. I dismounted and pulled it free. This was at Mina. News reached al-Hajjaj, and he began visiting him. Al-Hajjaj said, ‘If we knew who injured you!’ Ibn ‘Umar said, ‘You injured me.’ He said, ‘How?’ He said, ‘You carried weapons on a day when they were not carried, and you brought weapons into the Sanctuary when weapons were not brought into the Sanctuary.’

Comparison findings

  • The short al-Bara’ name remains as selected; Brill and the Bewley comparison expand it to ibn ‘Azib. The complete chain, addressed-us wording and Day of Sacrifice remain.
  • This occurrence explicitly says my maternal uncle and I slaughtered before praying. The source alternative Place it in its place—or he said, Slaughter it remains; neither comparison’s shortened conclusion replaces it. No fixed animal age is supplied.
  • The chapter introduction’s ‘Abdullah ibn Busr follows the unchanged Arabic in both prints. Bewley’s ibn Yusr is a comparison discrepancy. The Arabic introduction remains separate from this report, with its English translation pending.
  • Modern printed reference 968; Bewley 925 and pinned EPUB 2.15.85 were individually compared. The repeated account remains a separate complete entry.

Complete working English

Sulayman ibn Harb related to us. He said: Shu‘bah related to us, from Zubayd, from al-Sha‘bi, from al-Bara’. He said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, addressed us on the Day of Sacrifice. He said, ‘The first thing we begin with on this day of ours is to pray, then return and sacrifice. Whoever does that has followed our practice. Whoever slaughters before praying has merely hastened meat for his family; it is no part of the sacrifice.’ My maternal uncle, Abu Burdah ibn Niyar, stood and said, ‘Messenger of Allah, I slaughtered before praying, and I have a young she-goat better than a mature animal.’ He said, ‘Put it in its place’—or he said, ‘Slaughter it’—‘and a young she-goat will not suffice for anyone after you.’
