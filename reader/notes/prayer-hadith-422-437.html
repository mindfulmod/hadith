Complete working English

‘Ubayd ibn Isma‘il related to us. He said: Abu Usamah related to us, from Hisham, from his father, from ‘A’ishah:

A black slave woman belonged to an Arab clan. They set her free, and she stayed with them. She said: “A young girl of theirs went out wearing a red wishah made of leather strips.” She said: “She put it down, or it fell from her. A kite passed by it while it lay there, thought it was meat and snatched it.” She said: “They looked for it but did not find it.” She said: “They accused me of it.” She said: They began searching, until they even searched her private parts.

She said: “By Allah, I was standing with them when the kite passed by and dropped it.” She said: “It fell among them.” She said: “I said: ‘This is what you accused me of, as you claimed, while I am innocent of it. Here it is!’”

She said: She came to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and became a Muslim. ‘A’ishah said: She had a tent in the mosque, or a small hut. She said: She used to come to me and talk with me. She said: She never sat with me without saying:

“The day of the wishah is among the wonders of our Lord;

indeed, He rescued me from the town of unbelief.”

‘A’ishah said: I said to her: “Why is it that you never sit with me without saying this?” She said: She told me this account.
