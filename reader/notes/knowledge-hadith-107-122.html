Hadith 112

AI-assisted working translation. Independent review pending.

Open this hadith →

Comparison findings

  • The selected killing-or-elephant uncertainty belongs to Abu ‘Abdullah and is shown as a transmission note. The historical print reverses the alternatives and has a longer Muhammad/Abu Nu‘aym comment; neither replaces the selected wording. The selected riding animal is not narrowed to a she-camel.
  • The two final “except idhkhir” statements remain; the checked modern print has one. Abu so-and-so is not replaced by a name from another version. The qaf pronunciation note and the question about what was written remain; the historical text at this location and the abbreviated Khan-family comparison omit that closing note. Bewley calls the written item a letter, but the selected الخطبة is an address, not a letter.

AI-assisted comparison of the unchanged selected Arabic with two visually inspected Arabic prints and two individually matched English report-body comparisons. Variants, abbreviated chains and omissions are recorded rather than treated as exact agreement. Independent Arabic, English and context review remains pending.

Complete working English

Abu Nu‘aym al-Fadl ibn Dukayn related to us. He said: Shayban related to us, from Yahya, from Abu Salamah, from Abu Hurayrah:

Khuza‘ah killed a man from Banu Layth in the year of the conquest of Mecca, in retaliation for one of their people whom they had killed. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was informed of this, so he mounted his riding animal and delivered an address. He said: “Allah held back killing—or the elephant—from Mecca.”

Abu ‘Abdullah was uncertain.

“And He gave the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and the believers power over them. Indeed, it was not made lawful for anyone before me, nor has it been made lawful for anyone after me. Indeed, it was made lawful for me for an hour of a day. Indeed, at this very hour of mine it is sacred: its thorns are not to be cut, its trees are not to be cut down, and its fallen property is not to be picked up except by someone who announces it. If someone is killed, there is a choice between two courses: blood money is paid, or retaliation is carried out for the family of the person killed.”

A man from Yemen came and said: “Write it down for me, Messenger of Allah.” He said: “Write it for Abu so-and-so.” A man from Quraysh said: “Except idhkhir, Messenger of Allah, for we put it in our houses and graves.” The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “Except idhkhir, except idhkhir.”

Abu ‘Abdullah said: “Yuqad is said with the letter qaf.” Abu ‘Abdullah was asked: “What was written for him?” He said: “This address was written for him.”
