Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Maslamah related to us, from Malik, from Hisham ibn ‘Urwah, from his father, from ‘Aishah, who said:

The sun was eclipsed in the time of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, led the people in prayer. He stood and prolonged the standing, then bowed and prolonged the bowing. Then he stood and prolonged the standing, but it was shorter than the first standing. Then he bowed and prolonged the bowing, but it was shorter than the first bowing. Then he prostrated and prolonged the prostration. Then he did in the second rak‘ah as he had done in the first. Then he finished, and the sun had cleared. He addressed the people, praised Allah and extolled Him.

Then he said: “The sun and the moon are two of Allah’s signs. They are not eclipsed because of anyone’s death or life. When you see this, supplicate to Allah, proclaim His greatness, pray and give charity.”

Then he said: “O community of Muhammad, by Allah, no one has greater ghayrah than Allah concerning His male servant committing unlawful sexual intercourse or His female servant committing unlawful sexual intercourse. O community of Muhammad, by Allah, if you knew what I know, you would laugh little and weep much.”

Complete working English

Yahya ibn Bukayr related to us. He said: al-Layth related to me, from ‘Uqayl, from Ibn Shihab.

Another chain: Ahmad ibn Salih related to me. He said: ‘Anbasah related to us. He said: Yunus related to us, from Ibn Shihab: ‘Urwah related to me, from ‘Aishah, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, who said:

The sun was eclipsed during the life of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. He went out to the mosque, and the people formed rows behind him. He proclaimed Allah’s greatness, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, recited at length. Then he proclaimed Allah’s greatness and bowed for a long time. Then he said: “Allah hears whoever praises Him.” He stood and did not prostrate, and recited at length, but less than the first recitation. Then he proclaimed Allah’s greatness and bowed for a long time, but less than the first bowing. Then he said: “Allah hears whoever praises Him. Our Lord, to You belongs praise.” Then he prostrated. Then he said the like of that in the other rak‘ah, completing four bowings in four prostrations. The sun cleared before he finished.

Then he stood and praised Allah as He deserves. Then he said: “They are two of Allah’s signs. They are not eclipsed because of anyone’s death or life. When you see them, hasten to prayer.”

Kathir ibn ‘Abbas used to relate that ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Abbas, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, used to relate an account of the day the sun was eclipsed like ‘Urwah’s report from ‘Aishah. I said to ‘Urwah: “On the day it was eclipsed in Madinah, your brother did not pray more than two rak‘ahs, like the dawn prayer.” He said: “Yes, because he was mistaken about the Sunnah.”
