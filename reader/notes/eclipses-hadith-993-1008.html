Complete working English

‘Amr ibn ‘Awn related to us. He said: Khalid related to us, from Yunus, from al-Hasan, from Abu Bakrah, who said:

We were with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, when the sun was eclipsed. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, stood up, dragging his cloak, until he entered the mosque. We entered, and he led us in two rak‘ahs until the sun became clear. Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Maslamah related to us, from Malik, from Hisham ibn ‘Urwah, from his father, from ‘Aishah, who said:

The sun was eclipsed in the time of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, led the people in prayer. He stood and prolonged the standing, then bowed and prolonged the bowing. Then he stood and prolonged the standing, but it was shorter than the first standing. Then he bowed and prolonged the bowing, but it was shorter than the first bowing. Then he prostrated and prolonged the prostration. Then he did in the second rak‘ah as he had done in the first. Then he finished, and the sun had cleared. He addressed the people, praised Allah and extolled Him.

Then he said: “The sun and the moon are two of Allah’s signs. They are not eclipsed because of anyone’s death or life. When you see this, supplicate to Allah, proclaim His greatness, pray and give charity.”

Then he said: “O community of Muhammad, by Allah, no one has greater ghayrah than Allah concerning His male servant committing unlawful sexual intercourse or His female servant committing unlawful sexual intercourse. O community of Muhammad, by Allah, if you knew what I know, you would laugh little and weep much.”
