Complete working English

Musaddad related to us. He said: Yahya related to us, from Isma‘il. Qays related to us. He said: Jarir ibn ‘Abd Allah said to me:

We were with the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, when he looked at the moon on the night of the full moon and said: “You will indeed see your Lord as you see this. You will not suffer harm—or you will not be in doubt—in seeing Him. If you can avoid being overcome and missing a prayer before the sun rises and before it sets, then do so.” Then he said: “So glorify your Lord with praise before the rising of the sun and before its setting.”
