Complete working English

‘Uthman ibn Abi Shaybah related to us. He said: Jarir related to us, from Mansur, from Ibrahim, from al-Aswad, from ‘A’ishah, who said:

“Have you equated us with the dog and the donkey? I saw myself lying on the bed, and the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, would come, face the middle of the bed and pray. I disliked passing across in front of him, so I slipped out from the foot of the bed until I slipped out of my covering.”

Complete working English

Abu Ma‘mar related to us. He said: ‘Abd al-Warith related to us. He said: Yunus related to us, from Humayd ibn Hilal, from Abu Salih, that Abu Sa‘id said: The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

And Adam ibn Abi Iyas related to us. He said: Sulayman ibn al-Mughirah related to us. He said: Humayd ibn Hilal al-‘Adawi related to us. He said: Abu Salih al-Samman related to us. He said:

I saw Abu Sa‘id al-Khudri on a Friday praying toward something that screened him from the people. A young man from Banu Abi Mu‘ayt wanted to pass in front of him, so Abu Sa‘id pushed him in the chest. The young man looked but found no passage except in front of him. He tried to pass again, and Abu Sa‘id pushed him harder than the first time. He insulted Abu Sa‘id, then went in to Marwan and complained to him about what he had received from Abu Sa‘id. Abu Sa‘id entered after him to see Marwan, who said: “What is between you and your brother’s son, Abu Sa‘id?”

He said: I heard the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, say:

“When one of you prays toward something that screens him from the people and someone wants to pass in front of him, let him push him away. If he refuses, let him resist him with force, for he is a devil.”

Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from Abu al-Nadr, the client of ‘Umar ibn ‘Ubayd Allah, from Busr ibn Sa‘id:
