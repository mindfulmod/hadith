Complete working English

Ibrahim ibn Musa related to us. He said: Hisham ibn Yusuf informed us that Ibn Jurayj informed them. He said: Abu Bakr ibn Abi Mulaykah informed me, from ‘Uthman ibn ‘Abd al-Rahman al-Taymi, from Rabi‘ah ibn ‘Abdullah ibn al-Hudayr al-Taymi.

Abu Bakr said: Rabi‘ah was among the best of people.

Concerning what Rabi‘ah witnessed of ‘Umar ibn al-Khattab, may Allah Most High be pleased with him:

He recited Surat al-Nahl on the pulpit on Friday. When he reached the prostration passage, he came down and prostrated, and the people prostrated. On the following Friday he recited it, and when he reached the prostration passage he said, “O people, we come across passages of prostration. Whoever prostrates has acted rightly, and whoever does not prostrate incurs no sin.” ‘Umar, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, did not prostrate.

Nafi‘ added, from Ibn ‘Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both: “Indeed, Allah has not made prostration obligatory, except if we wish.”
