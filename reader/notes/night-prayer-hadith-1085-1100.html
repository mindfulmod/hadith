Complete working English

Hafs ibn ‘Umar related to us. He said: Khalid ibn ‘Abdullah related to us, from Husayn, from Abu Wa’il, from Hudhayfah, may Allah Most High be pleased with him:

When the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, rose for tahajjud during the night, he would clean his mouth with the siwak.
