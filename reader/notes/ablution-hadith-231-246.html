Complete working English

Sulayman ibn Harb related to us. He said: Hammad ibn Zayd related to us, from Ayyub, from Abu Qilabah, from Anas, who said:

Some people from ‘Ukl or ‘Uraynah came and found Madinah unhealthy. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, directed them to camels giving milk and to drink their urine and milk. They went, and when they recovered, they killed the herdsman of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and drove away the livestock. The news came at the beginning of the day, and he sent after them. When the day had advanced, they were brought. He gave the order, and their hands and feet were cut off, their eyes were pierced with nails, and they were cast into al-Harrah. They asked for water but were not given any.

Abu Qilabah said: “These people stole, killed, disbelieved after their belief, and waged war against Allah and His Messenger.”

Complete working English

‘Abdan related to us. He said: My father informed me, from Shu‘bah, from Abu Ishaq, from ‘Amr ibn Maymun, from ‘Abdullah, who said:

While the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was prostrating…

Another chain: He said: And Ahmad ibn ‘Uthman related to me. He said: Shurayh ibn Maslamah related to us. He said: Ibrahim ibn Yusuf related to us, from his father, from Abu Ishaq, who said: ‘Amr ibn Maymun related to me that ‘Abdullah ibn Mas‘ud related to him:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was praying at the House, while Abu Jahl and some companions of his were sitting there. Some said to others: “Which of you will bring the afterbirth of the camel of the people of so-and-so and put it on Muhammad’s back when he prostrates?” The most wretched of the people got up and brought it. He waited until the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prostrated, then placed it on his back between his shoulders. I was watching, unable to change anything. If only I had had protection! He said: They began laughing and leaning on one another, while the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, remained prostrating without raising his head, until Fatimah came to him and threw it off his back. He raised his head, then said: “O Allah, deal with Quraysh,” three times. It distressed them when he prayed against them. He said: They believed that supplication in that land was answered. Then he named them: “O Allah, deal with Abu Jahl, and deal with ‘Utbah ibn Rabi‘ah, Shaybah ibn Rabi‘ah, al-Walid ibn ‘Utbah, Umayyah ibn Khalaf and ‘Uqbah ibn Abi Mu‘ayt.” He named a seventh, but we did not remember him. Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Muqatil related to us. He said: ‘Abdullah informed us. He said: Sufyan informed us, from Mansur, from Sa‘d ibn ‘Ubaydah, from al-Bara’ ibn ‘Azib, who said: The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

“When you go to your bed, perform ablution as you do for the prayer, then lie down on your right side. Then say: ‘O Allah, I have surrendered my face to You, entrusted my affair to You, and sought support for my back with You, in hope and fear of You. There is no refuge and no escape from You except to You. O Allah, I believe in Your Book which You sent down, and in Your Prophet whom You sent.’ If you die that night, you will be upon the natural disposition. Make these the last words you speak.”

He said: I repeated them to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. When I reached “O Allah, I believe in Your Book which You sent down,” I said: “And Your Messenger.” He said: “No: ‘And Your Prophet whom You sent.’”
