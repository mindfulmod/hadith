Complete working English

Ahmad ibn Sa‘id related to us. He said: Habban related to us. He said: Hammam related to us. He said: Anas ibn Sirin related to us:

We went out to meet Anas when he arrived from al-Sham, and we met him at ‘Ayn al-Tamr. I saw him praying on a donkey, with his face towards this side, meaning to the left of the qiblah. I said, “I saw you praying in a direction other than the qiblah.” He said, “Had I not seen the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, do it, I would not have done it.”

Ibn Tahman related it from Hajjaj, from Anas ibn Sirin, from Anas, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.
