Hadith 885

AI-assisted working translation. Independent review pending.

Open this hadith →

Comparison findings

  • The digital أمه is explicitly corrected to أمة, supported by both prints. The shorter selected Isma‘il ibn Aban is retained; Brill adds al-Warraq. Brill has one shoulder and omits the honorific after Muhammad, whereas the selected text and modern print have both shoulders and the honorific. The community, not its geographical area, becomes fewer. Dark headcloth follows the lexical colour reading of دسمة discussed by al-‘Ayni; the oily-cloth wording in both English comparisons and alternative lexical explanations are documented, not silently treated as unanimous. No cause, illness detail or legal qualification is inserted. Modern reference: 927.

Complete working English

Isma‘il ibn Aban related to us. He said: Ibn al-Ghasil related to us. He said: ‘Ikrimah related to us, from Ibn ‘Abbas, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both:

He said: The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, ascended the pulpit. It was the last gathering in which he sat. He had wrapped a cloak around his shoulders and tied a dark headcloth around his head. He praised Allah and extolled Him, then said, ‘People, come to me.’ They gathered towards him. Then he said, ‘Now then. This community of the Ansar will become fewer, while the people become more numerous. Whoever takes charge of any part of the community of Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and is able in that position to harm someone or benefit someone, should accept from those among them who do good and pardon those among them who do wrong.’
