Hadith 91

AI-assisted working translation. Independent review pending.

Open this hadith →

Comparison findings

  • The selected digital chain ends the narrator’s name at ربيعة بن أبي عبد. Both printed witnesses continue it with الرحمن. The shorter selected form is retained as “Rabi‘ah ibn Abi ‘Abd”, with a visible name-difference notice; the missing component is not silently supplied. The historical print also names the preceding transmitter ‘Abd al-Malik ibn ‘Amr al-‘Aqadi where the selected and modern text use Abu ‘Amir.
  • Both “or he said” alternatives remain as transmission notes. وعاء and عفاص overlap in meaning; the provisional vessel/container rendering preserves both positions rather than collapsing the selected alternative. Ibn Hajar identifies wika’ as the tying material and ‘ifas as the receptacle.
  • The camel’s water-skin and footwear imagery is retained. Explanations about stored water or hooves are not inserted into the hadith. The one-year announcement, use, return if the owner comes, and all three animal answers remain; no modern legal advice is added.

Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Muhammad related to us. He said: Abu ‘Amir related to us. He said: Sulayman ibn Bilal al-Madini related to us, from Rabi‘ah ibn Abi ‘Abd, from Yazid, the mawla of al-Munba‘ith, from Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani:

A man asked the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, about a lost article. He said: “Recognize its tying cord—

Or he said: its vessel.

—and its container. Then announce it for a year, then make use of it. If its owner comes, return it to him.” He said: “What about a stray camel?” He became angry until his cheeks reddened—

Or he said: his face reddened.

—and said: “What have you to do with it? It has its water-skin and its footwear. It reaches water and grazes on trees, so leave it until its owner finds it.” He said: “What about a stray sheep?” He said: “For you, or your brother, or the wolf.”

Complete working English

Muhammad—he is Ibn Salam—informed us. Al-Muharibi related to us. He said: Salih ibn Hayyan related to us. He said: ‘Amir al-Sha‘bi said: Abu Burdah related to me, from his father, who said: The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

“Three have two rewards: a man from the People of the Book who believed in his prophet and believed in Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace; an enslaved man when he fulfils the right of Allah and the right of his masters; and a man who had an enslaved woman with whom he had intercourse, who trained her in manners and did so well, taught her and taught her well, then freed her and married her. Then ‘Amir said: “We have given it to you for nothing. People used to ride to Medina for less than this.”
