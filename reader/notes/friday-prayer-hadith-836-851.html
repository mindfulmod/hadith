‘Abdullah ibn Muhammad ibn Asma’ related to us. He said: Juwayriyah informed us, from Malik, from al-Zuhri, from Salim ibn ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Umar, from Ibn ‘Umar, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both:

Complete working English

‘Ali related to us. He said: Harami ibn ‘Umarah related to us. He said: Shu‘bah related to us, from Abu Bakr ibn al-Munkadir, who said: ‘Amr ibn Sulaym al-Ansari related to me:

He said: I testify that Abu Sa‘id said: I testify that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, “Bathing on Friday is obligatory for every male who has reached puberty, and cleaning the teeth with a toothstick, and applying perfume if it is available.”

‘Amr said: As for bathing, I testify that it is obligatory. As for cleaning the teeth and perfume, Allah knows best whether it is obligatory or not, but this is how it appears in the hadith.

Abu ‘Abdullah said: He is the brother of Muhammad ibn al-Munkadir. This Abu Bakr’s personal name was not given. Bukayr ibn al-Ashajj, Sa‘id ibn Abi Hilal and several others narrated this from him. Muhammad ibn al-Munkadir was known by the kunyahs Abu Bakr and Abu ‘Abdullah.

Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from Sumayy, the freedman of Abu Bakr ibn ‘Abd al-Rahman, from Abu Salih al-Samman, from Abu Hurayrah, may Allah Most High be pleased with him:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, “Whoever bathes on Friday with the bath for major ritual impurity and then goes, it is as though he has offered a camel. When the imam comes out, the angels attend to listen to the remembrance.”

Complete working English

Isma‘il related to us. He said: Sulayman ibn Bilal related to me. He said: Hisham ibn ‘Urwah said: My father informed me, from ‘A’ishah, may Allah Most High be pleased with her:

She said: ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr entered with a toothstick which he was using. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, looked at it. I said to him, “Give me this toothstick, ‘Abd al-Rahman.” He gave it to me, and I broke it, then chewed it, then gave it to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. He used it while leaning against my chest.
