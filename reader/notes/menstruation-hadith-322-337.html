Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn Yusuf related to us; Malik informed us, from ‘Abd Allah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn ‘Amr ibn Hazm, from his father, from ‘Amrah bint ‘Abd al-Rahman, from ‘A’ishah, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace:

That she said to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace: “Messenger of Allah, Safiyyah bint Huyayy has menstruated.” The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “Perhaps she will hold us back. Had she not circumambulated with you?” They said: “Yes.” He said: “Then leave.”

Complete working English

Mu‘alla ibn Asad related to us. He said: Wuhayb related to us, from ‘Abd Allah ibn Tawus, from his father, from Ibn ‘Abbas, who said:

Permission was given to the menstruating woman to depart if she menstruated.

At first, Ibn ‘Umar used to say that she should not depart. Then I heard him say: “She may depart. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, gave them permission.”

Comparison findings

  • Both inspected prints place an untitled باب before this report, numbered chapter 30. The digital source groups it under chapter 29’s funeral heading. A separately declared printed heading supplies only Chapter; it does not invent a descriptive title or change the original digital inventory. Fath al-Bari also records the presence and absence of this division among recensions.
  • The full chain retains Abu Awanah’s stated name al-Waddah, from his book and my maternal aunt. The historical print omits the name al-Waddah; the modern print agrees with the selected digital wording.
  • The selected report shifts from she to me at its close; this remains. Fath al-Bari explains beside and the place of prostration. The working English does not replace lying with the EPUB’s sitting or present the location as the mosque building. Small prayer mat renders khumrah without adding its material.

Complete working English

Al-Hasan ibn Mudrik related to us. He said: Yahya ibn Hammad related to us. He said: Abu ‘Awanah—whose name is al-Waddah—informed us from his book. He said: Sulayman al-Shaybani informed us, from ‘Abd Allah ibn Shaddad, who said: I heard my maternal aunt Maymunah, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace:

That she would be menstruating and not praying, while lying beside the place where the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prostrated. He would be praying on his small prayer mat. When he prostrated, some of his garment touched me.
