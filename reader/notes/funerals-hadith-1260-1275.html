Abu al-Nu‘man related to us: Jarir ibn Hazim related to us; he said: I heard Nafi‘ say:

Ibn ‘Umar was told that Abu Hurayrah, may Allah Most High be pleased with them, was saying, “Whoever follows a funeral has a qirat.” He said, “Abu Hurayrah has told us a great deal.” She confirmed—
