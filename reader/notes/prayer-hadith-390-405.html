Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn Raja’ related to us. He said: Isra’il related to us, from Abu Ishaq, from al-Bara’ ibn ‘Azib, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, who said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, had prayed towards Bayt al-Maqdis for sixteen or seventeen months. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, wished to be directed towards the Ka‘bah. Then Allah revealed: “We have certainly seen the turning of your face towards the sky.” He turned towards the Ka‘bah. The foolish among the people—who were the Jews—said: “What has turned them from the qiblah they had been facing? Say: To Allah belong the east and the west. He guides whom He wills to a straight path.” A man prayed with the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, then went out after praying. He passed some of the Ansar in the ‘Asr prayer, facing Bayt al-Maqdis. He said that he testified that he had prayed with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and that he had faced towards the Ka‘bah. The people turned until they faced towards the Ka‘bah.

Complete working English

‘Uthman related to us. He said: Jarir related to us, from Mansur, from Ibrahim, from ‘Alqamah, who said: ‘Abd Allah said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prayed.

Ibrahim said: I do not know whether he added or omitted.

When he gave the salutation, he was asked: “Messenger of Allah, has something new happened in the prayer?” He said: “What is that?” They said: “You prayed such-and-such.” He bent his legs, faced the qiblah and performed two prostrations, then gave the salutation. When he turned his face towards us, he said: “If something new had happened in the prayer, I would have informed you of it. But I am only a human being like you; I forget as you forget. So when I forget, remind me. If any of you is uncertain in his prayer, let him seek what is correct and complete it on that basis, then give the salutation, then perform two prostrations.”

‘Amr ibn ‘Awn related to us. He said: Hushaym related to us, from Humayd, from Anas, who said: ‘Umar said:

I agreed with my Lord regarding three matters. I said to them: “Perhaps his Lord, if he divorces you, will give him wives better than you in your place.” Then this verse was revealed.
