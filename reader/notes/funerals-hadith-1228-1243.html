‘Abd Allah ibn Yusuf related to us: Malik informed us, from Ibn Shihab, from ‘Amir ibn Sa‘d ibn Abi Waqqas, from his father, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, who said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to visit me in the year of the Farewell Pilgrimage because of an illness that had become severe. I said, “The illness has brought me to this state. I have wealth, and only a daughter inherits from me. Shall I give two-thirds of my wealth in charity?” He said, “No.” I said, “Half?” He said, “No.” Then he said, “A third, and a third is great—or much. To leave your heirs wealthy is better than to leave them dependent, begging from people. You will not spend anything seeking thereby the Face of Allah without being rewarded for it, even what you put into your wife’s mouth.” I said, “Messenger of Allah, will I be left behind after my companions?” He said, “You will not be left behind and do a righteous deed without thereby increasing in degree and elevation. Then perhaps you will be left behind, so that some people benefit through you and others are harmed through you. Allah, complete my companions’ emigration for them, and do not turn them back on their heels. But poor Sa‘d ibn Khawlah!”

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, grieved for him because he died in Makkah.
