Complete working English

Ya‘qub ibn Ibrahim related to us. Ibn ‘Ulayyah related to us. Ayyub informed us, from Nafi‘:

Ibn ‘Umar, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, did not pray Duha except on two days: the day he arrived in Mecca, for he used to arrive there in the forenoon, circumambulate the House and then pray two rak‘ahs behind the Maqam; and the day he came to the Quba Mosque. He used to come to it every Saturday. When he entered the mosque, he disliked leaving it until he had prayed in it. He said: He used to relate that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, would visit it riding and on foot.

He said: He used to say, “I only do as I saw my companions doing. I do not prevent anyone from praying at whatever hour he wishes of the night or day, except that you should not deliberately seek out sunrise or sunset.”

Abu al-Walid related to us. Shu‘bah related to us, from ‘Abd al-Malik: I heard Qaza‘ah, the mawla of Ziyad, who said:

I heard Abu Sa‘id al-Khudri, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, relate four things from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, which pleased and delighted me. He said:

A woman is not to travel for two days unless her husband or a mahram is with her. And there is no fasting on two days: al-Fitr and al-Adha. And there is no prayer after two prayers: after the morning prayer until the sun rises, and after ‘Asr until it sets. And journeys are not to be undertaken except to three mosques: the Sacred Mosque, al-Aqsa Mosque and my mosque.
