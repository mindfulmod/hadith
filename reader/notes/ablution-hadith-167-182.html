‘Abdullah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from Ishaq ibn ‘Abdullah ibn Abi Talhah, from Anas ibn Malik, who said:

I saw the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, when the time for the ‘Asr prayer had come. The people looked for water for ablution but did not find any.
