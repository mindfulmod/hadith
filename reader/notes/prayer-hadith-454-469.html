Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Sinan related to us. He said: Fulayh related to us. He said: Abu al-Nadr related to us, from ‘Ubayd ibn Hunayn, from Busr ibn Sa‘id, from Abu Sa‘id al-Khudri, who said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, delivered an address and said: “Allah gave a servant the choice between this world and what is with Him, and he chose what is with Allah.” Abu Bakr, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, wept. I said to myself: “What makes this old man weep if Allah gave a servant the choice between this world and what is with Him, and he chose what is with Allah?” The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was that servant, and Abu Bakr was the most knowledgeable of us. He said: “Abu Bakr, do not weep. The person most generous to me with his companionship and wealth is Abu Bakr. If I were to take a close friend from my community, I would take Abu Bakr. But there is the brotherhood of Islam and its affection. Let no door in the mosque remain without being closed, except the door of Abu Bakr.”

Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Ju‘fi related to us. He said: Wahb ibn Jarir related to us. He said: My father related to us. He said: I heard Ya‘la ibn Hakim, from ‘Ikrimah, from Ibn ‘Abbas, who said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, came out during the illness in which he died, with a cloth wrapped around his head. He sat on the pulpit, praised Allah and extolled Him. Then he said: “No one has been more generous to me with himself and his wealth than Abu Bakr ibn Abi Quhafah. If I were to take a close friend from among the people, I would take Abu Bakr as a close friend. But the friendship of Islam is better. Close every small passage door in this mosque for me, except the small passage door of Abu Bakr.”

Abu al-Nu‘man and Qutaybah related to us. They both said: Hammad related to us, from Ayyub, from Nafi‘, from Ibn ‘Umar:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, arrived in Makkah and called for ‘Uthman ibn Talhah.
