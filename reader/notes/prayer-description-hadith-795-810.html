Complete working English

Abu al-Yaman related to us. He said: Shu‘ayb informed us, from al-Zuhri, who said: ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn Hurmuz related to me—a client of Banu ‘Abd al-Muttalib (on one occasion he said: a client of Rabi‘ah ibn al-Harith)—from ‘Abdullah ibn Buhaynah, who was from Azd Shanu’ah, was an ally of Banu ‘Abd Manaf and was one of the companions of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace:

Abu al-Yaman related to us. He said: Shu‘ayb informed us, from al-Zuhri, who said: ‘Urwah ibn al-Zubayr informed us, from ‘A’ishah, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, who informed him:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to supplicate in prayer: “O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the punishment of the grave, and I seek refuge in You from the trial of the false messiah, and I seek refuge in You from the trial of life and the trial of death. Muhammad ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Sufyan related to us, from ‘Abd al-Malik ibn ‘Umayr, from Warrad, the secretary of al-Mughirah ibn Shu‘bah, who said:

Al-Mughirah ibn Shu‘bah dictated to me in a letter to Mu‘awiyah that the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to say after every obligatory prayer: “There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. His is the dominion, and His is the praise, and He has power over all things. O Allah, none can withhold what You give, and none can give what You withhold, and wealth does not benefit the wealthy person against You.”

Shu‘bah said, from ‘Abd al-Malik, with this report; from al-Hakam, from al-Qasim ibn Mukhaymirah, from Warrad, with this report. Al-Hasan said: Jadd means wealth.

Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Maslamah related to us, from Malik, from Salih ibn Kaysan, from ‘Ubaydullah ibn ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Utbah ibn Mas‘ud, from Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani, who said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, led us in the dawn prayer at al-Hudaybiyah after rain during the night. When he finished, he turned towards the people and said, “Do you know what your Lord said?” They said, “Allah and His messenger know best.” He said, “Among My servants this morning are a believer in Me and a disbeliever. As for whoever said, ‘We were given rain by Allah’s favour and mercy,’ that person believes in Me and disbelieves in the star.
