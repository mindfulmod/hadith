Isma‘il related to us; he said: Malik related to me, from Hisham, from his father, from ‘A’ishah, may Allah Most High be pleased with her, who said:

When the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was ill, some of his wives mentioned a church they had seen in the land of al-Habashah, called Mariyah. Muhammad ibn Sinan related to us: Fulayh ibn Sulayman related to us: Hilal ibn ‘Ali related to us, from Anas, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, who said:

We attended a daughter of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was sitting by the grave, and I saw his eyes shedding tears. ‘Abd Allah ibn Yusuf related to us: Al-Layth related to us; he said: Ibn Shihab related to me, from ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn Ka‘b ibn Malik, from Jabir ibn ‘Abd Allah, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, who said:
