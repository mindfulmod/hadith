Complete working English

Abu Nu‘aym related to us. He said: Shayban related to us, from Yahya, who said: I heard Abu Salamah say: Jabir ibn ‘Abd Allah informed us:

That ‘Umar ibn al-Khattab came to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, on the Day of the Trench and said: “Messenger of Allah, by Allah, I had scarcely managed to pray before the sun was about to set.” That was after the fasting person had broken his fast. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “By Allah, I have not prayed it.” The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, went down to Buthan, and I was with him.
