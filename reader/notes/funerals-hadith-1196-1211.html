Muhammad related to us. ‘Abd al-Wahhab al-Thaqafi related to us, from Ayyub, from Muhammad, from Umm ‘Atiyyah, may Allah Most High be pleased with her, who said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, came in to us while we were washing his daughter. He said, “Wash her three or five times, or more than that, with water and lote leaves. Put camphor in the last washing. When you have finished, let me know.” When we had finished, we let him know. He cast his waist-wrapper to us and said, “Place it next to her body.”

Ayyub said: Hafsah also related to me the like of Muhammad’s hadith. Hafsah’s hadith included: “Wash her an odd number of times.” It included: “Three or five or seven.” It included that he said: “Begin with her right side and her places of ablution.” It included that Umm ‘Atiyyah said: “We combed her hair into three braids.”
