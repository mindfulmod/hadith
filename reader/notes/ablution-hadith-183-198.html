Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from ‘Amr ibn Yahya al-Mazini, from his father:

A man said to ‘Abdullah ibn Zayd, who was the grandfather of ‘Amr ibn Yahya: “Can you show me how the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to perform ablution?” ‘Abdullah ibn Zayd said: “Yes.” He called for water, poured it over his hands and washed twice. Then he rinsed his mouth and expelled water from his nose three times. Then he washed his face three times. Then he washed each of his hands up to the elbows twice. Then he wiped his head with both hands, moving them forward and back. He began at the front of his head, took them to his nape, then returned them to the place where he had begun. Then he washed his feet.

Musa related to us. He said: Wuhayb related to us, from ‘Amr, from his father:

I witnessed ‘Amr ibn Abi Hasan

asking ‘Abdullah ibn Zayd about the ablution of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. Adam related to us. He said: Shu‘bah related to us. He said: Al-Hakam related to us. He said: I heard Abu Juhayfah say:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, came out to us in the midday heat. Water for ablution was brought to him and he performed ablution. Khalid ibn Makhlad related to us. He said: Sulayman related to us. He said: ‘Amr ibn Yahya related to me, from his father, who said:

My uncle used to perform ablution a great deal.
