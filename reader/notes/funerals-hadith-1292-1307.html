Complete working English

Abu al-Yaman related to us: Shu‘ayb informed us. Ibn Shihab said:

“Prayer is offered over every newborn who dies, even one born of unlawful intercourse, because he was born upon the natural disposition of Islam, if his parents profess Islam—or his father alone, even if his mother follows something other than Islam. If he cries out at birth, prayer is offered over him. Prayer is not offered over one who does not cry out, because he is a miscarried child.”

For Abu Hurayrah, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, used to relate: The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

“No newborn is born except upon the natural disposition. Then his parents make him a Jew, a Christian or a Magian, just as an animal gives birth to a whole animal. Do you perceive any with parts cut off?”

Then Abu Hurayrah, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, says: “Allah’s natural disposition upon which He created people”—the verse.
