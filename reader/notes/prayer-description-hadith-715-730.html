Complete working English

Isma‘il related to us. He said: Malik related to me, from Zayd ibn Aslam, from ‘Ata’ ibn Yasar, from ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Abbas, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, who said:

The sun was eclipsed during the time of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and he prayed. They said, “Messenger of Allah, we saw you reach for something in the place where you stood, then we saw you move back.” He said, “I was shown Paradise and reached for a cluster from it. Had I taken it, you would have eaten from it for as long as the world remained.”

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Sinan related to us. He said: Fulayh related to us. He said: Hilal ibn ‘Ali related to us, from Anas ibn Malik, who said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, led us in prayer, then ascended the pulpit and gestured with both hands towards the mosque’s direction of prayer. Then he said, “Just now, since I led you in prayer, I saw Paradise and the Fire represented on the prayer-facing side of this wall. I have never seen the like of today in good and evil”—three times.
