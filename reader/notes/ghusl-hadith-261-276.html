Musa ibn Isma‘il related to us. He said: Abu ‘Awanah related to us. Al-A‘mash related to us, from Salim ibn Abi al-Ja‘d, from Kurayb, the freedman of Ibn ‘Abbas, from Ibn ‘Abbas, from Maymunah bint al-Harith, who said:

Muhammad ibn Bashshar related to us. He said: Mu‘adh ibn Hisham related to us. He said: My father related to me, from Qatadah, who said: Anas ibn Malik related to us. He said:

Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn Maslamah related to us, from Malik, from Abu al-Nadr, the freedman of ‘Umar ibn ‘Ubayd Allah, that Abu Murrah, the freedman of Umm Hani’ bint Abi Talib, informed him that he heard Umm Hani’ bint Abi Talib saying:

I went to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, in the year of the conquest and found him bathing while Fatimah screened him. He said: “Who is this?” I said: “I am Umm Hani’.”
