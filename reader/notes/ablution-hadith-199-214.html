‘Abdullah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from Yahya ibn Sa‘id, from Bashir ibn Yasar, the freedman of Banu Harithah, that Suwayd ibn al-Nu‘man informed him:

Ya‘qub ibn Ibrahim related to us. He said: Isma‘il ibn Ibrahim related to us. He said: Rawh ibn al-Qasim related to me. He said: ‘Ata’ ibn Abi Maymunah related to me, from Anas ibn Malik, who said:

When the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, went out to relieve himself, I would bring him water with which he would wash himself.
