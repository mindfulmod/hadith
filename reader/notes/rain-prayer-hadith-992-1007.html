Comparison findings

  • The selected source and both prints read the singular miftah (key). The English comparisons use plural keys. The singular is retained with the five-item predicate; a separate note explains this source wording without silently normalising the Arabic. Al-‘Ayni records a plural variant in another transmission.
  • All five clauses remain, including what will be in the wombs rather than only the sex of an unborn child. No claims about modern prediction are added.
  • Brill expands Ibn ‘Umar to ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Umar and uses Prophet where the selected source and modern print have Messenger of Allah. The selected names and title remain.

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Sufyan related to us, from ‘Abdullah ibn Dinar, from Ibn ‘Umar, who said: The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

“The key to the unseen is five things that none knows except Allah: no one knows what will be tomorrow; no one knows what will be in the wombs; no soul knows what it will gain tomorrow; no soul knows in which land it will die; and no one knows when the rain will come.”
