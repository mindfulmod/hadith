Complete working English

‘Ali ibn ‘Abdullah related to us. He said: Sufyan related to us, from ‘Amr, who said: Kurayb informed me, from Ibn ‘Abbas:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, slept until he snored, then prayed.

Sometimes he said: “He lay down until he snored, then got up and prayed.”

Then Sufyan related to us this report time after time, from ‘Amr, from Kurayb, from Ibn ‘Abbas, who said:

One night I stayed with my maternal aunt Maymunah. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, got up during the night. When part of the night had passed, the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, got up and performed a light ablution from a hanging worn waterskin.

‘Amr described it as light and small in amount.

He stood to pray. I performed ablution much as he had, then came and stood on his left.

Sometimes Sufyan said: “On his left.”

He moved me and placed me on his right. Then he prayed as much as Allah willed, then lay down and slept until he snored. Then the caller came and informed him of the prayer. He got up with him to go to the prayer, prayed, and did not perform ablution.

We said to ‘Amr: “Some people say that the eye of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, sleeps, but his heart does not sleep.” ‘Amr said: “I heard ‘Ubayd ibn ‘Umayr say: ‘The dreams of the prophets are revelation.’” Then he recited: {I see in a dream that I am slaughtering you.}

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn ‘Abd al-Rahim related to us. He said: Abu Salamah al-Khuza‘i, Mansur ibn Salamah, informed us. He said: Ibn Bilal—meaning Sulayman—informed us, from Zayd ibn Aslam, from ‘Ata’ ibn Yasar, from Ibn ‘Abbas:

He performed ablution and washed his face. Then he took a handful of water and rinsed his mouth and drew water into his nose with it. Then he took a handful of water and did this with it: he added it to his other hand and washed his face with both. Then he took a handful of water and washed his right hand and arm with it. Then he took a handful of water and washed his left hand and arm with it. Then he wiped his head. Then he took a handful of water and sprinkled it on his right foot until he had washed it. Then he took another handful and washed his foot with it—meaning the left. Then he said: “This is how I saw the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, perform ablution.”
