Complete working English

‘Umar ibn Hafs related to us. He said: My father related to us. He said: Al-A‘mash related to us. He said: Abu Salih related to me, from Abu Hurayrah, who said: The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Bashshar related to us. He said: Yahya related to us, from ‘Ubayd Allah, who said: Khubayb ibn ‘Abd al-Rahman related to me, from Hafs ibn ‘Asim, from Abu Hurayrah, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, who said:

“Seven will Allah shade in His shade on a day when there is no shade except His shade: a just imam; a young person who grew up worshipping his Lord; a man whose heart is attached to the mosques; two men who loved one another for Allah’s sake, meeting on that basis and parting on that basis; a man whom a woman of rank and beauty sought, who said, ‘I fear Allah’; a man who gave charity and concealed it so that his left hand did not know what his right hand spent; and a man who remembered Allah alone, and his eyes overflowed.”

Complete working English

Qutaybah related to us. He said: Isma‘il ibn Ja‘far related to us, from Humayd, who said:

And Anas was asked: “Did the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, have a ring?”

He said: “Yes. One night he delayed the ‘Isha’ prayer until halfway through the night. Then, after he had prayed, he turned his face towards us and said: ‘The people have prayed and slept, and you have continued to be in prayer since you began waiting for it.’” He said: “It is as though I am looking at the gleam of his ring.”

Hadith 631

AI-assisted working translation. Independent review pending.

Open this hadith →

Comparison findings

  • The selected digital and modern print use and in the first morning/evening clause, then or in the final each-time clause. The historical print uses or in both; the selected distinction remains. Bewley renders nuzul as residence and the Khan-family text combines place and hospitality. Ibn al-Mulaqqin explains the term as hospitality prepared for a guest, and records both time-of-day and broader going/returning senses. This working translation selects morning/evening and hospitality; no palace, size of reward or additional instruction is supplied.

Complete working English

‘Ali ibn ‘Abd Allah related to us. He said: Yazid ibn Harun related to us. He said: Muhammad ibn Mutarrif informed us, from Zayd ibn Aslam, from ‘Ata’ ibn Yasar, from Abu Hurayrah, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, who said:

“Whoever goes to the mosque in the morning and in the evening, Allah prepares hospitality for him in Paradise each time he goes in the morning or the evening.”
