Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Bashshar related to us. He said: Muhammad ibn Ja‘far related to us. He said: Shu‘bah related to us, from ‘Ata’ ibn Abi Maymunah, who heard Anas ibn Malik saying:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, would enter the privy, and a boy and I would carry a small water vessel and an ‘anazah. He would cleanse himself with the water.

Al-Nadr and Shadhan followed him in transmitting this from Shu‘bah. An ‘anazah is a staff with an iron tip.

Complete working English

Abu Nu‘aym related to us. He said: Zuhayr related to us, from Abu Ishaq, who said: It was not Abu ‘Ubaydah who mentioned it, but ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Aswad, from his father, that he heard ‘Abdullah saying:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, went to relieve himself and ordered me to bring him three stones. I found two stones and looked for the third, but could not find it. ‘Abdullah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from Sa‘id al-Maqburi, from ‘Ubayd ibn Jurayj, that he said to ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Umar:

“Abu ‘Abd al-Rahman, I have seen you do four things which I have not seen any of your companions do.” He said: “What are they, Ibn Jurayj?” He said: “I have seen you touch none of the corners except the two Yemeni ones. I have seen you wear tanned-leather sandals, and I have seen you dye with yellow. I have seen that, when you are in Makkah, the people begin the talbiyah when they see the crescent, but you do not begin it until the Day of Tarwiyah.”

‘Abdullah said: “As for the corners, I did not see the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, touch any except the two Yemeni ones. As for tanned-leather sandals, I saw the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, wear sandals with no hair on them and perform ablution while wearing them, so I like to wear them. As for the yellow, I saw the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, dye with it, so I like to dye with it. As for beginning the talbiyah, I did not see the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, begin it until his mount rose with him.”
