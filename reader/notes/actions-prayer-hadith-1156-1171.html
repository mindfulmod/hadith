Complete working English

Abu Ma‘mar related to us. ‘Abd al-Warith related to us. Kathir ibn Shinzir related to us, from ‘Ata’ ibn Abi Rabah, from Jabir ibn ‘Abdullah, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, who said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, sent me on an errand for him. I went, then returned after completing it. I came to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and greeted him, but he did not reply to me. Something came into my heart which Allah knows best. I said to myself, “Perhaps the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, is angry with me because I was slow in returning to him.” Then I greeted him, but he did not reply to me, and something more intense than the first time came into my heart. Then I greeted him, and he replied to me. He said, “What prevented me from replying to you was that I was praying.” He was on his riding animal, facing a direction other than the qiblah.

Complete working English

Qutaybah related to us. ‘Abd al-‘Aziz related to us, from Abu Hazim, from Sahl ibn Sa‘d, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, who said:

It reached the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, that something had happened among Banu ‘Amr ibn ‘Awf at Quba. He went out with some of his companions to reconcile them. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was delayed, and the time for prayer came. Bilal came to Abu Bakr, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, and said, “O Abu Bakr, the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, has been delayed, and the time for prayer has come. Would you lead the people?” He said, “Yes, if you wish.” Bilal called the iqamah for prayer. Abu Bakr, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, went forward and pronounced the takbir for the people. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, came walking through the rows, making his way through them, until he stood in the row. The people began clapping.

Sahl said: Tasfih is tasfiq—clapping.

He said: Abu Bakr, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, did not turn around during his prayer. When the people increased it, he turned, and there was the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. He signalled to him, directing him to pray. Abu Bakr, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, raised his hand and praised Allah. Then he stepped backwards behind him until he stood in the row. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, went forward and prayed for the people. When he finished, he turned towards the people and said, “O people, why, when something happened to you in prayer, did you begin clapping? Clapping is only for women. Whoever has something happen to him in his prayer should say, ‘Glory be to Allah.’”

Then he turned to Abu Bakr, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, and said, “O Abu Bakr, what prevented you from praying for the people when I signalled to you?” Abu Bakr said, “It was not fitting for the son of Abu Quhafah to pray in front of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.”
