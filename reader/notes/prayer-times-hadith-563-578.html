Complete working English

‘Imran ibn Maysarah related to us. He said: Muhammad ibn Fudayl related to us. He said: Husayn related to us, from ‘Abd Allah ibn Abi Qatadah, from his father, who said:

We travelled with the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, one night. Some of the people said: “If only you would stop for us to rest, Messenger of Allah.” He said: “I fear that you will sleep through the prayer.” Bilal said: “I will wake you.” They lay down, and Bilal rested his back against his mount. His eyes overcame him and he slept. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, awoke when the edge of the sun had appeared. He said: “Bilal, where is what you said?” He said: “Never has sleep like this come upon me.” He said: “Allah took your souls when He wished and returned them to you when He wished.
