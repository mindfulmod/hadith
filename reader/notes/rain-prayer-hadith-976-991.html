Complete working English

And Abu Nu‘aym said to us, from Zuhayr, from Abu Ishaq:

‘Abdullah ibn Yazid al-Ansari went out, and al-Bara’ ibn ‘Azib and Zayd ibn Arqam went out with him, may Allah Most High be pleased with them. Complete working English

Ayyub ibn Sulayman said: Abu Bakr ibn Abi Uways related to me, from Sulayman ibn Bilal. Yahya ibn Sa‘id said: I heard Anas ibn Malik say:

A Bedouin man from the desert people came to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, on Friday and said, “O Messenger of Allah, the livestock have perished, the dependants have perished, the people have perished.” The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, raised his hands, supplicating, and the people raised their hands with him, supplicating.

Muhammad related to us. He said: ‘Abdullah informed us. He said: Al-Awza‘i informed us. He said: Ishaq ibn ‘Abdullah ibn Abi Talhah al-Ansari related to us. He said: Anas ibn Malik related to me. He said:

Complete working English

Isma‘il related to us. Malik related to me, from Salih ibn Kaysan, from ‘Ubaydullah ibn ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Utbah ibn Mas‘ud, from Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani, who said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, led us in the dawn prayer at al-Hudaybiyah after rain during the night. When the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, finished, he turned towards the people and said, “Do you know what your Lord said?” They said, “Allah and His messenger know best.”

He said, “Among My servants this morning are a believer in Me and a disbeliever. As for whoever said, ‘We were given rain by Allah’s favour and mercy,’ that person believes in Me and disbelieves in the star. But whoever said, ‘By the naw’ of such-and-such,’ that person disbelieves in Me and believes in the star.”
