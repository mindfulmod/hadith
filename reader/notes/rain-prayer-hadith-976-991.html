Complete working English

And Abu Nu‘aym said to us, from Zuhayr, from Abu Ishaq:

‘Abdullah ibn Yazid al-Ansari went out, and al-Bara’ ibn ‘Azib and Zayd ibn Arqam went out with him, may Allah Most High be pleased with them. He sought rain, standing on his feet before them, without a pulpit. He sought forgiveness, then prayed two rak‘ahs, reciting aloud. No adhan or iqamah was given.

Abu Ishaq said: ‘Abdullah ibn Yazid had seen the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.

AI-assisted working translation. Independent review pending.

Comparison findings

  • The complete selected report ends with the traveller and the blocked road. No later supplication or outcome is borrowed from parallel accounts, and the separate al-Uwaysi report is not merged into it.
  • The working rendering unable to proceed follows the impeded-travel sense discussed by Ibn Hajar; Ibn Rajab also records disagreement and variant readings. This does not establish one uncontested lexical meaning. The selected bashiq wording remains unchanged.
  • Brill adds a short lexical gloss after the report, uses the Messenger of Allah instead of Prophet of Allah at the second visit, and expands with him to with the Messenger of Allah with an additional honorific. The modern print agrees with the selected shorter report and does not include that gloss. None of those additions is silently inserted.
  • Dependants remains broader than children. Each of the three perishing clauses and each of the three selected honorifics remains, as does the repeated description of the man as a Bedouin from the desert people.
  • Modern printed reference 1029. Complete relevant Arabic print pages and the located English bodies were inspected. This is AI-assisted working English, not Darussalam wording.

Complete working English

Ayyub ibn Sulayman said: Abu Bakr ibn Abi Uways related to me, from Sulayman ibn Bilal. Yahya ibn Sa‘id said: I heard Anas ibn Malik say:

A Bedouin man from the desert people came to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, on Friday and said, “O Messenger of Allah, the livestock have perished, the dependants have perished, the people have perished.” The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, raised his hands, supplicating, and the people raised their hands with him, supplicating.

He said: We had not left the mosque before rain fell upon us. Rain continued to fall upon us until the following Friday. The man came to the Prophet of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and said, “O Messenger of Allah, the traveller is unable to proceed and the road is blocked.”
