Complete working English

Muhammad ibn ‘Abd Allah ibn Hawshab related to us: ‘Abd al-Wahhab related to us: Yahya ibn Sa‘id related to us; he said: ‘Amrah informed me; she said: I heard ‘A’ishah, may Allah Most High be pleased with her, say:

When news of the killing of Zayd ibn Harithah, Ja‘far and ‘Abd Allah ibn Rawahah came, the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, sat down with grief apparent in him, while I was looking through the crack in the door. A man came to him and said, “Messenger of Allah, the women of Ja‘far”—and he mentioned their weeping. He ordered him to forbid them. The man went, then came and said, “I have forbidden them,” and mentioned that they had not obeyed him. He ordered him a second time to forbid them. He went, then came and said, “By Allah, they have indeed overcome me—or overcome us.”

The uncertainty is from Muhammad ibn Hawshab.

She stated that the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

“Then cast dust into their mouths.” I said, “May Allah rub your nose in the dust! By Allah, you are not going to do it, and you have not spared the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, the trouble.”
