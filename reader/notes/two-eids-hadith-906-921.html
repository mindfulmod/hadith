Complete working English

Abu al-Yaman related to us. He said: Shu‘ayb informed us, from al-Zuhri. He said: Salim ibn ‘Abdullah informed me that ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Umar said:

‘Umar took a robe of thick silk that was being sold in the market. He took it and came to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and said, ‘Messenger of Allah, buy this and adorn yourself with it for Eid and for delegations.’ The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said to him, ‘This is only the clothing of one who has no share.’ ‘Umar remained as long as Allah willed him to remain. Then the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, sent him a robe of silk brocade. ‘Umar came with it and brought it to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. He said, ‘Messenger of Allah, you said, “This is only the clothing of one who has no share,” yet you have sent me this robe.’ The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said to him, ‘Sell it, or meet your need with it.’

Complete working English

Ahmad related to us. He said: Ibn Wahb related to us. He said: ‘Amr informed us that Muhammad ibn ‘Abd al-Rahman al-Asadi related to him, from ‘Urwah, from ‘A’ishah. She said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, came in to me while two girls were with me, singing the songs of Bu‘ath. He lay down on the bed and turned his face away. Abu Bakr came in and rebuked me, saying, ‘A pipe of Satan in the presence of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace!’ The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, turned towards him and said, ‘Leave them both.’ When he became inattentive, I signalled to the two of them, and they went out.

It was an Eid day, and the Black people were playing with shields and spears. Either I asked the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, or he said, ‘Would you like to watch?’ I said, ‘Yes.’ He stood me behind him, my cheek against his cheek, while he was saying, ‘Carry on, Banu Arfidah!’ When I grew tired, he said, ‘Is that enough for you?’ I said, ‘Yes.’ He said, ‘Then go.’

Complete working English

‘Uthman related to us. He said: Jarir related to us, from Mansur, from al-Sha‘bi, from al-Bara’ ibn ‘Azib, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both. He said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, addressed us on the day of Adha after the prayer. He said, ‘Whoever prays our prayer and performs our sacrifice has fulfilled the sacrifice. Will it suffice for me?’ He said, ‘Yes, but it will not suffice for anyone after you.’
