Complete working English

Abu al-Nu‘man related to us. Hammad ibn Zayd related to us, from Ayyub, from Nafi‘, from Ibn ‘Umar, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, who said:

During the time of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, I saw as though a piece of brocade was in my hand. It was as though, whenever I wished for a place in Paradise, it flew to it. Hafsah recounted one of my dreams to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, “What a fine man ‘Abdullah is! If only he prayed at night.” ‘Abdullah, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, would then pray at night.

They kept recounting dreams to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, that it was on the seventh night of the last ten. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, “I see that your dreams agree on the last ten. Whoever seeks it should seek it among the last ten.”

Complete working English

Qutaybah related to us. He said: ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi al-Mawali related to us, from Muhammad ibn al-Munkadir, from Jabir ibn ‘Abdullah, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, who said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to teach us istikharah in matters as he taught us a surah of the Quran. He would say, “When one of you intends a matter, let him pray two rak‘ahs apart from the obligatory prayer, then say: O Allah, I seek Your choice through Your knowledge and seek ability through Your power, and I ask You of Your great bounty. You have power and I do not; You know and I do not, and You are the Knower of the unseen. O Allah, if You know that this matter is good for me in my religion, my livelihood, and the outcome of my affair (or he said: the immediate and later part of my affair), then ordain it for me, make it easy for me, and then bless it for me. If You know that this matter is bad for me in my religion, my livelihood, and the outcome of my affair (or he said: in the immediate and later part of my affair), then turn it away from me and turn me away from it. Ordain good for me wherever it may be, then make me content with it.”
