Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from Ibn Shihab, from Sa‘id ibn al-Musayyab and Abu Salamah ibn ‘Abd al-Rahman, who both informed him, from Abu Hurayrah:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, “When the imam says Amin, say Amin, for whoever’s saying of Amin coincides with the angels’ saying of Amin will have his previous sins forgiven.”
