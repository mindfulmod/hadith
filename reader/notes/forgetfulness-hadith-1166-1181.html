Complete working English

Hafs ibn ‘Umar related to us. Yazid ibn Ibrahim related to us, from Muhammad, from Abu Hurayrah, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, who said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prayed one of the two afternoon prayers—Muhammad said: I think it was most likely ‘Asr—as two rak‘ahs, then gave the greeting of peace.

Then he stood up and went to a piece of wood at the front of the mosque and placed his hand on it. Abu Bakr and ‘Umar, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, were among them, but they were too awed to speak to him. Those who were hurrying left and said, “Has the prayer been shortened?” A man whom the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, called Dhu al-Yadayn said, “Did you forget, or has it been shortened?” He said, “I did not forget, and it has not been shortened.” He said, “Yes, you have forgotten.”

So he prayed two rak‘ahs, then gave the greeting of peace. Then he pronounced the takbir and prostrated as he usually prostrated, or longer. Then he raised his head and pronounced the takbir. Then he lowered his head and pronounced the takbir and prostrated as he usually prostrated, or longer. Then he raised his head and pronounced the takbir.

Complete working English

Isma‘il related to us. He said: Malik related to me, from Hisham, from his father, from ‘A’ishah, may Allah Most High be pleased with her, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, who said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prayed in his house while ill, seated. Some people prayed behind him standing, so he signalled to them to sit down. When he finished, he said, “The imam has only been appointed to be followed. When he bows, bow, and when he rises, rise.”
