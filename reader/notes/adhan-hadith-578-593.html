Complete working English

Mahmud ibn Ghaylan related to us. He said: ‘Abd al-Razzaq related to us. He said: Ibn Jurayj informed us. He said: Nafi‘ informed me that Ibn ‘Umar used to say:

When the Muslims arrived in Madinah, they would gather and estimate the time of prayer; no call was made for it. One day they discussed this. Some of them said: “Use a bell like the Christians’ bell.” Others said: “Rather, a trumpet like the Jews’ horn.” ‘Umar said: “Will you not send a man to call to prayer?” The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “Bilal, stand up and call to prayer.”

Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A‘raj, from Abu Hurayrah:

That the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “When the call to prayer is made, Satan turns away, passing wind, so that he does not hear the adhan. Complete working English

Qutaybah ibn Sa‘id related to us. He said: Isma‘il ibn Ja‘far related to us, from Humayd, from Anas ibn Malik:

Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from Nafi‘, from ‘Abd Allah ibn ‘Umar, who said: Hafsah informed me:

That when the caller remained in place for the dawn prayer and dawn had appeared, the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, would pray two brief rak‘ahs before the prayer was commenced.
