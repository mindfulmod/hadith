Complete working English

Mu‘adh ibn Asad related to us. He said: Al-Fadl ibn Musa informed me. He said: Sa‘id ibn ‘Ubayd al-Ta’i informed us, from Bashir ibn Yasar al-Ansari, from Anas ibn Malik:

That he came to Madinah and was asked: “What have you disapproved of among us since the time you knew the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace?” He said: “I have disapproved of nothing except that you do not straighten the rows.”

Complete working English

Muhammad related to us. He said: ‘Abdah informed us, from Yahya ibn Sa‘id al-Ansari, from ‘Amrah, from ‘A’ishah, who said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to pray at night in his room. The room’s wall was low, and the people saw the figure of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. Some people stood and followed his prayer. In the morning they spoke about that. He stood the second night, and some people stood with him, following his prayer. They did that for two or three nights. After that, the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, remained seated and did not come out. In the morning the people mentioned that. He said: “I feared that the night prayer would be prescribed for you.”

Complete working English

‘Abd al-A‘la ibn Hammad related to us. He said: Wuhayb related to us. He said: Musa ibn ‘Uqbah related to us, from Salim Abu al-Nadr, from Busr ibn Sa‘id, from Zayd ibn Thabit:

That the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, made a small room—

He said: “I think he said: of a mat”—

in Ramadan. He prayed in it for several nights, and some of his Companions followed his prayer. When he became aware of them, he began to remain seated. He went out to them and said: “I have understood what I saw you doing. People, pray in your houses, for the best prayer is a person’s prayer in his house, except the prescribed prayer.”

‘Affan said: Wuhayb related to us. Musa related to us: I heard Abu al-Nadr, from Busr, from Zayd, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.
