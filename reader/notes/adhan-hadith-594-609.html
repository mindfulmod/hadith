Complete working English

Abu Nu‘aym related to us. He said: Shayban related to us, from Yahya, from Abu Salamah, from ‘A’ishah:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to pray two brief rak‘ahs between the call and the iqamah for the dawn prayer.

Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn Yusuf related to us. Malik informed us, from ‘Abd Allah ibn Dinar, from ‘Abd Allah ibn ‘Umar:

That the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “Bilal makes the call at night, so eat and drink until Ibn Umm Maktum makes the call.”
