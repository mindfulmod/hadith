Hadith 596

AI-assisted working translation. Independent review pending.

Comparison findings

  • All three source alternatives remain: any one of you/or anyone among you, gives the adhan/or calls, dawn/or morning. The finger movements and Zuhayr’s own demonstration remain. The Khan-family comparison labels the last demonstrator al-Zuhri; the selected Arabic and both prints name Zuhayr. Its added explanation of the horizon is not inserted. Al-‘Ayni explains the dawn phrase as a gesture, not merely an announcement that morning has begun. The historical print has an extra or before the waking clause; the selected digital and modern reading are retained.

Complete working English

Ahmad ibn Yunus related to us. He said: Zuhayr related to us. He said: Sulayman al-Taymi related to us, from Abu ‘Uthman al-Nahdi, from ‘Abd Allah ibn Mas‘ud, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, who said:

“Let Bilal’s adhan not prevent any one of you—or anyone among you—from his pre-dawn meal, for he gives the adhan—or calls—at night, so that those of you standing in prayer may return and those of you sleeping may awaken. Dawn—or morning—is not like this”—and he gestured with his fingers, raising them upwards and lowering them downwards—“until it is like this.”

And Zuhayr gestured with his two index fingers, one above the other, then extended them to his right and left.

Abu Nu‘aym related to us. He said: Shayban related to us, from Yahya, from ‘Abd Allah ibn Abi Qatadah, from his father, who said:
