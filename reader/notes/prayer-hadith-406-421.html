Complete working English

Malik ibn Isma‘il related to us. He said: Zuhayr related to us. He said: Humayd related to us, from Anas:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, saw phlegm in the direction of the qiblah and scraped it off with his hand. His dislike was seen—or his dislike of it and how distressing it was to him were seen. He said: “When one of you stands in his prayer, he is conversing privately with his Lord—or his Lord is between him and his qiblah. Complete working English

And Ibrahim said, from ‘Abd al-‘Aziz ibn Suhayb, from Anas, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, who said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was brought wealth from Bahrayn. He said: “Spread it out in the mosque.” It was the greatest amount of wealth that had been brought to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, went out to the prayer without looking towards it. When he had finished the prayer, he came and sat beside it. There was no one he saw without giving him something.

Then al-‘Abbas came to him and said: “Messenger of Allah, give me some, for I paid the ransom for myself and paid the ransom for ‘Aqil.” The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said to him: “Take.” He scooped some into his garment, then tried to lift it but could not. He said: “Messenger of Allah, tell one of them to lift it up to me.” He said: “No.” He said: “Then lift it onto me yourself.” He said: “No.” So he poured out some of it, then tried to lift it. He said: “Messenger of Allah, tell one of them to lift it onto me.” He said: “No.” He said: “Then lift it onto me yourself.” He said: “No.” So he poured out some of it, then lifted it, placed it on his shoulders and went away.

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, kept following him with his gaze until he disappeared from our sight, astonished at his eagerness. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, did not get up while a dirham of it remained there.
