Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Muhammad related to us. ‘Uthman ibn ‘Umar related to us. Yunus informed us, from al-Zuhri, who said: Hind bint al-Harith related to me that Umm Salamah, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, informed her:

In the time of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, the women would rise when they had said the closing greeting of the obligatory prayer. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and the men who had prayed remained as long as Allah willed. When the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, rose, the men rose.
