Someone came to Ibn ‘Umar and said to him: “Here is the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace; he has entered the Ka‘bah.” Ibn ‘Umar said: I came, but the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, had already come out.
