Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Muhammad related to us. He said: Hisham related to us. Ma‘mar informed us, from al-Zuhri and Hisham ibn ‘Urwah, from ‘Urwah, from ‘Aishah, may Allah Most High be pleased with her, who said:

The sun was eclipsed in the time of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, stood and led the people in prayer. He prolonged the recitation. Then he bowed and prolonged the bowing. Then he raised his head and prolonged the recitation, but it was less than his first recitation. Then he bowed and prolonged the bowing, but less than his first bowing. Then he raised his head and prostrated twice. Then he stood and did the like of that in the second rak‘ah.

Then he stood and said: “The sun and the moon are not eclipsed because of anyone’s death or life. They are, however, two of Allah’s signs which He shows His servants. When you see that, hasten to prayer.”

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Mihran related to us. He said: al-Walid related to us. He said: Ibn Namir informed us; he heard Ibn Shihab, from ‘Urwah, from ‘Aishah, may Allah Most High be pleased with her:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, recited aloud in the eclipse prayer. When he finished his recitation, he proclaimed Allah’s greatness and bowed. When he rose from the bowing, he said: “Allah hears whoever praises Him. Our Lord, and to You belongs praise.” Then he resumed recitation. In the eclipse prayer there were four bowings in two rak‘ahs and four prostrations.

Al-Awza‘i and others said: I heard al-Zuhri, from ‘Urwah, from ‘Aishah, may Allah Most High be pleased with her, that:

The sun was eclipsed during the time of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. He sent a caller to announce: “Prayer in congregation.” Then he went forward and prayed four bowings in two rak‘ahs and four prostrations.

And ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn Namir informed me that he heard the same from Ibn Shihab.

Al-Zuhri said: I said: “Your brother, ‘Abdullah ibn al-Zubayr, did not do that. He prayed only two rak‘ahs, like the dawn prayer, when he prayed in Madinah.” He said: “Yes, he was mistaken about the Sunnah.”

Sufyan ibn Husayn and Sulayman ibn Kathir followed him in transmitting from al-Zuhri about reciting aloud.
