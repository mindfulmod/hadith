Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Muhammad related to us. He said: Hisham related to us. Ma‘mar informed us, from al-Zuhri and Hisham ibn ‘Urwah, from ‘Urwah, from ‘Aishah, may Allah Most High be pleased with her, who said:

The sun was eclipsed in the time of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, stood and led the people in prayer. He prolonged the recitation. Then he bowed and prolonged the bowing. Then he raised his head and prolonged the recitation, but it was less than his first recitation. Then he bowed and prolonged the bowing, but less than his first bowing. Then he raised his head and prostrated twice. Then he stood and did the like of that in the second rak‘ah.

Then he stood and said: “The sun and the moon are not eclipsed because of anyone’s death or life. They are, however, two of Allah’s signs which He shows His servants. When you see that, hasten to prayer.”

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn al-‘Ala’ related to us. He said: Abu Usamah related to us, from Burayd ibn ‘Abdullah, from Abu Burdah, from Abu Musa, who said:

The sun was eclipsed, and the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, stood up in alarm, fearing that it might be the Hour. He came to the mosque and prayed with the longest standing, bowing and prostration I had ever seen him perform.

He said: “These signs which Allah sends do not occur because of anyone’s death or life, but {Allah makes His servants fear through them}. When you see anything of that, hasten to remember Him, supplicate to Him and seek His forgiveness.”
