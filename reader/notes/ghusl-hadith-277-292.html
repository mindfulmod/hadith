Complete working English

‘Abdan related to us. He said: ‘Abd Allah informed us. He said: Sufyan informed us, from al-A‘mash, from Salim ibn Abi al-Ja‘d, from Kurayb, from Ibn ‘Abbas, from Maymunah, who said:

I screened the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, while he was bathing from janabah. He washed his hands, then poured with his right hand onto his left and washed his private parts and what had touched them. Then he wiped his hand on the wall or the ground. Then he performed his ablution for prayer except for his feet. Then he poured water over his body, then moved aside and washed his feet.

Abu ‘Awanah and Ibn Fudayl corroborated it regarding the screening.
