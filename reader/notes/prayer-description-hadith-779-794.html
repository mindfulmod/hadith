Complete working English

Musa related to us. He said: Hammam related to us, from Yahya, from Abu Salamah, who said:

I went to Abu Sa‘id al-Khudri and said, “Will you not come out with us to the palm trees so we can talk?” He went out. He said: I said, “Relate to me what you heard from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, about the Night of Decree.” He said: The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, observed retreat during the first ten of Ramadan, and we observed retreat with him. Jibril came to him and said, “What you seek lies ahead of you.” Then he observed retreat during the middle ten, and we observed retreat with him. Jibril came to him and said, “What you seek lies ahead of you.” The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, stood to address the people on the morning of the twentieth of Ramadan. He said, “Whoever observed retreat with the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, should return. I was shown the Night of Decree, and I forgot it. It is among the last ten, on an odd night. I saw as though I were prostrating in mud and water.” The mosque’s roof was palm branches, and we saw nothing in the sky. Then a cloud came, and rain fell on us. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, led us in prayer until I saw the trace of mud and water on the forehead of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and the tip of his nose, confirming his dream.

Yahya ibn Bukayr related to us. He said: Al-Layth related to us, from Khalid, from Sa‘id, from Muhammad ibn ‘Amr ibn Halhalah, from Muhammad ibn ‘Amr ibn ‘Ata’.

And al-Layth related to us, from Yazid ibn Abi Habib and Yazid ibn Muhammad, from Muhammad ibn ‘Amr ibn Halhalah, from Muhammad ibn ‘Amr ibn ‘Ata’:

He was sitting with a group of the companions of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. We mentioned the prayer of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. Abu Humayd al-Sa‘idi said, “I remembered the prayer of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, better than any of you. I saw that when he said the takbir, he put his hands level with his shoulders. When he bowed, he placed his hands firmly on his knees, then bent his back. When he raised his head, he stood upright until every vertebra returned to its place. When he prostrated, he placed his hands down, neither spreading them out flat nor drawing them in close, and pointed the tips of his toes towards the qiblah. When he sat after the two rak‘ahs, he sat on his left foot and set the right upright. When he sat in the last rak‘ah, he brought his left foot forward, set the other upright and sat on his buttocks.”

Al-Layth heard Yazid ibn Abi Habib; Yazid heard Muhammad ibn Halhalah; and Ibn Halhalah heard Ibn ‘Ata’. Abu Salih said, from al-Layth: “Every vertebra.” Ibn al-Mubarak said, from Yahya ibn Ayyub, who said: Yazid ibn Abi Habib related to me that Muhammad ibn ‘Amr related to him: “Every vertebra.”
