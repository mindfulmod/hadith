Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from Hisham ibn ‘Urwah, from his father, from ‘A’ishah, may Allah Most High be pleased with her, who said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to pray thirteen rak‘ahs at night, then, when he heard the call to the dawn prayer, pray two brief rak‘ahs.

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Bashshar related to us. He said: Muhammad ibn Ja‘far related to us. Shu‘bah related to us, from Muhammad ibn ‘Abd al-Rahman, from his aunt ‘Amrah, from ‘A’ishah, may Allah Most High be pleased with her, who said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to—

Another route: Ahmad ibn Yunus related to us. Zuhayr related to us. Yahya—he is Ibn Sa‘id—related to us, from Muhammad ibn ‘Abd al-Rahman, from ‘Amrah, from ‘A’ishah, may Allah Most High be pleased with her, who said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to make the two rak‘ahs before the dawn prayer brief, so that I would say, “Did he recite the Mother of the Book?”

Unchanged Arabic report

[1118] حدثنا محمد بن بشار قال: حدثنا محمد بن جعفر حدثنا شعبة عن محمد بن عبد الرحمن عن عمته عمرة عن عائشة رضى الله تعالى عنها قالت كان النبي ﷺ ح وحدثنا أحمد بن يونس حدثنا زهير حدثنا يحيى هو ابن سعيد عن محمد بن عبد الرحمن عن عمرة عن عائشة رضى الله تعالى عنها قالت كان النبي ﷺ يخفف الركعتين اللتين قبل صلاة الصبح حتى إني لأقول هل قرأ بأم الكتاب
