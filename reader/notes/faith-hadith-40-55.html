  • Zuhayr’s final addition begins with its own Abu Ishaq transmission and is retained as a transmission note, including the deaths, killings and Qur’anic excerpt. It is not dropped as a duplicated chain.
  • The final Qur’anic excerpt corresponds to 2:143, checked against https://quran.com/2/143. The separate chapter introduction glosses faith as prayer; that introduction remains Arabic context, not an addition to the quoted verse or numbered report.

Complete working English

‘Amr ibn Khalid related to us. He said: Zuhayr related to us. He said: Abu Ishaq related to us, from al-Bara’:

When the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, first came to Madinah, he stayed with his grandfathers—or he said his maternal uncles—from the Ansar. He prayed facing Bayt al-Maqdis for sixteen months or seventeen months. He desired that his direction of prayer should be towards the House. The first prayer he prayed was the afternoon prayer, and a group prayed with him. A man who had prayed with him went out and passed people in a mosque while they were bowing. He said: “I testify by Allah that I have prayed with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, facing Makkah.” They turned, just as they were, towards the House.

The Jews had been pleased when he prayed facing Bayt al-Maqdis, as had the People of the Book. When he turned his face towards the House, they objected to that.

Zuhayr said: Abu Ishaq related to us, from al-Bara’, in this report of his, that men had died facing the direction of prayer before it was changed, and others had been killed. We did not know what to say about them. Then Allah Most High revealed: {Allah would not let your faith go to waste}.

Complete working English

Isma‘il related to us. He said: Malik ibn Anas related to me, from his uncle Abu Suhayl ibn Malik, from his father, that he heard Talhah ibn ‘Ubaydullah say:

A man from the people of Najd came to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, with dishevelled hair. “Five prayers in the day and night.” He said: “Is anything else required of me?” He said: “No, unless you do so voluntarily.” The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “And fasting Ramadan.” He said: “Is anything else required of me?” He said: “No, unless you do so voluntarily.”

He said: The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, mentioned zakat to him. He said: “Is anything else required of me?” He said: “No, unless you do so voluntarily.” He said: The man turned away saying: “By Allah, I will add nothing to this and take nothing away.” The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “He has succeeded, if he is truthful.”
