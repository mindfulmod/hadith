Complete working English

Bishr ibn Muhammad related to us. He said: ‘Abdullah informed us. He said: Yunus informed us, from al-Zuhri, who said: Salim ibn ‘Abdullah informed us, from Ibn ‘Umar, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, “Each of you is a shepherd.”

Al-Layth added: Yunus said: Ruzayq ibn Hukaym wrote to Ibn Shihab, while I was with him that day in Wadi al-Qura, “Do you think I should hold the Friday prayer?” Ruzayq was in charge of land which he cultivated, and on it was a group of Black people and others. Ruzayq was then in charge of Aylah. Ibn Shihab wrote, while I was listening, commanding him to hold the Friday prayer and informing him that Salim had related to him that ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Umar said: I heard the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, say:

“Each of you is a shepherd, and each of you is responsible for his flock. The imam is a shepherd and is responsible for his flock. A man is a shepherd in his household, and he is responsible for his flock. A woman is a shepherd in her husband’s house and is responsible for her flock. A servant is a shepherd over his master’s property and is responsible for his flock.”

He said: I think he also said:

“A man is a shepherd over his father’s property and is responsible for his flock. Each of you is a shepherd and is responsible for his flock.”
