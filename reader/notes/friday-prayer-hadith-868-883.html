Complete working English

‘Abdan related to us. He said: ‘Abdullah informed us. He said: Ibn Abi Dhi’b informed us, from Sa‘id al-Maqburi, from his father, from Ibn Wadi‘ah, from Salman al-Farisi:

Complete working English

Ibn Muqatil related to us. He said: ‘Abdullah informed us. He said: Abu Bakr ibn ‘Uthman ibn Sahl ibn Hunayf informed us, from Abu Umamah ibn Sahl ibn Hunayf:
