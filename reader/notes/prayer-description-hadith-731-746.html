Complete working English

Adam related to us. He said: Shu‘bah related to us. He said: Sayyar ibn Salamah related to us. He said:

My father and I visited Abu Barzah al-Aslami and asked him about the times of the prayers. He said: The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to pray the noon prayer when the sun declined, and the afternoon prayer when a man could return to the furthest part of Madinah while the sun was still bright.

I have forgotten what he said about the sunset prayer.

He did not mind delaying the night prayer until a third of the night. He did not like sleeping before it or conversation afterwards. He would pray the dawn prayer and a man would leave able to recognize the person sitting beside him. He used to recite between sixty and a hundred in the two rak‘ahs—or in one of them.
