Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Yusuf related to us. Malik informed us, from Makhramah ibn Sulayman, from Kurayb, the mawla of Ibn ‘Abbas, that he informed him, from ‘Abdullah ibn ‘Abbas, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both:

He spent the night with Maymunah, Mother of the Believers, may Allah Most High be pleased with her, who was his maternal aunt. He said: I lay down across the pillow, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and his wife lay along its length. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, slept until midnight, or a little before it, or a little after it. Then the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, awoke, sat up and wiped the sleep from his face with his hand. Then he recited the ten verses at the end of Surat Al ‘Imran. Then he went to a hanging worn waterskin and performed ablution from it, performing his ablution well. Then he stood to pray.

‘Abdullah ibn ‘Abbas, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, said: I got up and did as he had done. Then I went and stood beside him. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, placed his right hand on my head and took my right ear, twisting it with his hand. He prayed two rak‘ahs, then two rak‘ahs, then two rak‘ahs, then two rak‘ahs, then two rak‘ahs, then two rak‘ahs, then prayed Witr. Then he lay down until the caller to prayer came to him. He got up and prayed two light rak‘ahs, then went out and prayed the morning prayer.

Complete working English

‘Abdullah ibn Maslamah related to us. ‘Abd al-‘Aziz ibn Abi Hazim related to us, from his father, from Sahl, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, who said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, went out to reconcile Banu ‘Amr ibn ‘Awf, and the time for prayer came. Bilal came to Abu Bakr, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, and said, “The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, has been delayed. Will you lead the people?” He said, “Yes, if you wish.” Bilal called the iqamah for prayer. Abu Bakr, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, went forward and prayed. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, came walking through the rows, making his way through them, until he stood in the first row. The people began clapping.

Sahl said: Do you know what tasfih is? It is tasfiq—clapping.

Abu Bakr, may Allah Most High be pleased with him, did not turn around during his prayer. When they increased it, he turned, and there was the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, in the row. He signalled to him, “Stay where you are.” Abu Bakr raised his hands and praised Allah. Then he stepped backwards behind him, and the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, went forward and prayed.

Complete working English

Sulayman ibn Harb related to us. Hammad related to us, from Ayyub, from Nafi‘, from Ibn ‘Umar, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, saw phlegm on the qiblah side of the mosque and became angry with the people of the mosque. He said, “Allah is before each of you. When he is in his prayer, he must not spit”—or he said, “must not expectorate.” Then he came down and scraped it off with his hand.

Ibn ‘Umar, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both, said: When one of you spits, let him spit to his left.
