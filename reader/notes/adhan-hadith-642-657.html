Complete working English

‘Ubayd ibn Isma‘il related to us, from Abu Usamah, from ‘Ubayd Allah, from Nafi‘, from Ibn ‘Umar, who said: The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

“When supper is set out for one of you and the iqamah has been given, begin with supper, and he should not hurry until he has finished it.”

Food would be set out for Ibn ‘Umar and the iqamah would be given, but he would not go to it until he had finished, while he could hear the imam reciting.

And Zuhayr and Wahb ibn ‘Uthman said: From Musa ibn ‘Uqbah, from Nafi‘, from Ibn ‘Umar, who said: The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said:

“When one of you is eating, he should not hurry until he has satisfied his need for it, even if the iqamah has been given.”

Ibrahim ibn al-Mundhir related it from Wahb ibn ‘Uthman, and Wahb is from Madinah.

Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from Hisham ibn ‘Urwah, from his father, from ‘A’ishah, Mother of the Believers, may Allah Most High be pleased with her, that she said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said during his illness: “Tell Abu Bakr to lead the people in prayer.” ‘A’ishah said: I said: “If Abu Bakr stands in your place, he will not make the people hear because of his weeping, so tell ‘Umar to lead the people in prayer.” ‘A’ishah said: I said to Hafsah: “Say to him: ‘If Abu Bakr stands in your place, he will not make the people hear because of his weeping, so tell ‘Umar to lead the people in prayer.’” Hafsah did so. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “Stop! You are indeed the women associated with Yusuf. Tell Abu Bakr to lead the people in prayer.” Hafsah said to ‘A’ishah: “I was never to get any good from you.”
