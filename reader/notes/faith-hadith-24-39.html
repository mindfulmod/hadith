Complete working English

Abu al-Yaman related to us: Shu‘ayb informed us, from al-Zuhri, who said: ‘Amir ibn Sa‘d ibn Abi Waqqas informed me, from Sa‘d, may Allah Most High be pleased with him:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, gave to a group of men while Sa‘d was sitting there. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, left out a man who was the one I admired most. I said, “Messenger of Allah, why have you passed over so-and-so? By Allah, I regard him as a believer.” He said, “Or a Muslim.” I was silent for a little while, but then what I knew about him overcame me, and I repeated my words. I said, “Why have you passed over so-and-so? By Allah, I regard him as a believer.” He said, “Or a Muslim.” Then what I knew about him overcame me, and I repeated my words, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, repeated his. Then he said, “Sa‘d, I give to a man while another is dearer to me than he is, for fear that Allah may cast him headlong into the Fire.”

Yunus, Salih, Ma‘mar and the son of al-Zuhri’s brother also related it from al-Zuhri.
