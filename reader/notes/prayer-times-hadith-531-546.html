Complete working English

‘Abd al-‘Aziz ibn ‘Abd Allah related to us. He said: Ibrahim related to me, from Ibn Shihab, from Salim ibn ‘Abd Allah, from his father, who informed him that he heard the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, say:

“Your remaining duration, compared with the nations that came before you, is only like the interval between the ‘Asr prayer and sunset. The people of the Torah were given the Torah and worked until midday, when they were unable to continue, and were given a qirat each. Then the people of the Gospel were given the Gospel and worked until the ‘Asr prayer, when they were unable to continue, and were given a qirat each. Then we were given the Qur’an and worked until sunset, and were given two qirats each. The people of the two Books said: ‘Our Lord, You gave these two qirats each and gave us one qirat each, although we did more work.’” He said: “Allah, mighty and majestic, said: ‘Have I wronged you in any part of your wages?’ They said: ‘No.’ He said: ‘It is My favour; I give it to whomever I will.’”
