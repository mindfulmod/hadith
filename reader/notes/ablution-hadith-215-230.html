Complete working English

Muhammad ibn al-Muthanna related to us. He said: Muhammad ibn Khazim related to us. He said: Al-A‘mash related to us, from Mujahid, from Tawus, from Ibn ‘Abbas, who said:

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, passed by two graves and said: “They are being punished, but they are not being punished for something great. One of them did not guard himself from urine, and the other used to go about carrying tales.” Then he took a fresh palm stalk, split it into two halves, and planted one in each grave. They asked: “Messenger of Allah, why did you do this?” He said: “Perhaps their punishment will be lightened as long as the two do not dry out.”

Ibn al-Muthanna said: And Waki‘ related to us. He said: Al-A‘mash related to us. He said: I heard Mujahid relate the same: “guard himself from his urine.”
