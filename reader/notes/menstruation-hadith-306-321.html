Comparison findings

  • The compiler’s explicit alternative between Ayyub and Hisham ibn Hassan remains between chain segments, followed by Umm Atiyyah’s attribution to the Prophet. The historical print has a shorter opening without this alternative; the modern print agrees with the selected fuller version. No or is silently inserted between kust and azfar, and no generic light perfume replaces the selected words. These terms need independent review.
  • All time periods and both prohibitions surrounding the exception remain. The selected final ننهي spelling is retained in Arabic rather than silently normalized; the passive working English follows the context and inspected prints.

Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn ‘Abd al-Wahhab related to us. He said: Hammad ibn Zayd related to us, from Ayyub, from Hafsah.

Abu ‘Abd Allah said: “Or Hisham ibn Hassan, from Hafsah.”

From Umm ‘Atiyyah, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. She said:

We were forbidden to mourn a dead person for more than three days, except for a husband: four months and ten days. We were not to apply kohl, use perfume or wear a dyed garment, except an ‘asb garment. When becoming pure, if one of us bathed after her menstruation, we were allowed a small amount of kust azfar. We were forbidden to follow funeral processions.

He said: Hisham ibn Hassan related it from Hafsah, from Umm ‘Atiyyah, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.

Complete working English

Yahya related to us. He said: Ibn ‘Uyaynah related to us, from Mansur ibn Safiyyah, from his mother, from ‘A’ishah:

That a woman asked the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, about her washing after menstruation. He instructed her how to bathe. He said: “Take a piece of cloth with musk and purify yourself with it.” She said: “How do I purify myself?” He said: “Purify yourself with it.” She said: “How?” He said: “Glory be to Allah! Purify yourself.” I pulled her towards me and said: “Follow the trace of blood with it.”

Complete working English

Muslim related to us. He said: Wuhayb related to us; Mansur related to us, from his mother, from ‘A’ishah:

That a woman of the Ansar said to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace: “How do I bathe after menstruation?” He said: “Take a piece of cloth scented with musk and clean yourself three times.” Then the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, felt embarrassed and turned his face away.

Or he said: “Clean yourself with it.”

I took hold of her, pulled her towards me and told her what the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, meant.

Complete working English

Musa ibn Isma‘il related to us; Ibrahim related to us; Ibn Shihab related to us, from ‘Urwah, that ‘A’ishah said:

I entered ihram with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, during the Farewell Hajj. I was among those performing tamattu‘ who had not brought the sacrificial animals.

She stated that she menstruated and did not become pure until the night of ‘Arafah began. She said: “Messenger of Allah, this is the night of ‘Arafah, and I had only begun tamattu‘ with an ‘umrah.” The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said to her: “Undo your hair and comb it, and hold back from your ‘umrah.”

I did so. When I had completed the Hajj, on the night of al-Hasbah he instructed ‘Abd al-Rahman, who took me to perform ‘umrah from al-Tan‘im in place of my ‘umrah whose rites I had begun.

Complete working English

‘Ubayd ibn Isma‘il related to us. He said: Abu Usamah related to us, from Hisham, from his father, from ‘A’ishah, who said:

We set out at the appearance of the crescent of Dhu al-Hijjah. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: “Whoever wishes to enter ihram for ‘umrah, let him do so. Had I not brought the sacrificial animals, I would have entered ihram for ‘umrah.” Some entered ihram for ‘umrah and some for Hajj. I was among those who entered ihram for ‘umrah. The day of ‘Arafah came upon me while I was menstruating, so I complained to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. He said: “Leave your ‘umrah, undo your hair and comb it, and enter ihram for Hajj.” I did so. When it was the night of al-Hasbah, he sent my brother ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr with me. I went out to al-Tan‘im and entered ihram for ‘umrah in place of my ‘umrah.

Hisham said: There was no sacrificial offering, fasting or charity in any of that.

Complete working English

Musaddad related to us. He said: Hammad related to us, from ‘Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Bakr, from Anas ibn Malik, from the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, who said:
