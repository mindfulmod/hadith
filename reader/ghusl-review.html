Complete working English

‘Abd Allah ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Malik informed us, from Hisham, from his father, from ‘A’ishah, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace:

Whenever the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, bathed for janabah, he began by washing his hands. Then he would perform ablution as he performed ablution for prayer. Then he would put his fingers into the water and work them through the roots of his hair. Then he would pour three handfuls over his head with his two hands, then pour water over all his skin.

Complete working English

Muhammad ibn Yusuf related to us. He said: Sufyan related to us, from al-A‘mash, from Salim ibn Abi al-Ja‘d, from Kurayb, from Ibn ‘Abbas, from Maymunah, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, who said:

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, performed his ablution for prayer except for his feet, and washed his private parts and whatever had been soiled. Then he poured water over himself, then moved his feet aside and washed them. This was his washing for janabah.

Opening quotations

The original Arabic is preserved below. English translation and independent review are pending. This material is available while browsing and is not part of a numbered reading or listening session.

وقول الله تعالى { وإن كنتم جنبا فاطهروا وإن كنتم مرضى أو على سفر أو جاء أحد منكم من الغائط أو لمستم النساء فلم تجدوا ماء فتيمموا صعيدا طيبا فامسحوا بوجوهكم وأيديكم منه ما يريد الله ليجعل عليكم من حرج ولكن يريد ليطهركم وليتم نعمته عليكم لعلكم تشكرون } وقوله جل ذكره { يا أيها الذين آمنوا لا تقربوا الصلاة وأنتم سكارى حتى تعلموا ما تقولون ولا جنبا إلا عابري سبيل حتى تغتسلوا وإن كنتم مرضى أو على سفر أو جاء أحد منكم من الغائط أو لمستم النساء فلم تجدوا ماء فتيمموا صعيدا طيبا فامسحوا بوجوهكم وأيديكم إن الله كان عفوا غفورا }
