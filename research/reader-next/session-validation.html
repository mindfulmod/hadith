The selected session reader

18 October 2026. B's finite session, A's quiet reading and C's listening view are now combined in the production reader. The user selected the current session with Continue as the returning view.

What changed

  • The home screen shows the two-entry session, reading place, listening action and offline readiness.
  • Reading keeps each narration whole, with a compact artwork strip, expandable complete narrator chain, source status and a bottom source action.
  • A dedicated listening view uses the same persistent audio element and continuous Cedar recording. Outside that view, the player becomes compact.
  • Explicit read-and-continue actions advance a finite session. Bookmarks, reading marks and listening positions stay separate.

Observed checks

  • All eight rendered English and Arabic blocks across the two entries matched the canonical source exactly.
  • At 393 × 852 and 21px text, closed narrator chain: the first narration starts at about 367px from document top, versus about 687px in the previous baseline. This is a layout measurement, not a readability score.
  • The two-hadith reading flow reached its end state with explicit read marks. A bookmark survived navigation, restart and reload.
  • Source dialogs opened for the requested entry, closed with Escape and restored focus to their trigger.
  • Audio advanced from Hadith 1 into Hadith 2 while the reading page remained on Hadith 1. The source dialog followed the reading entry there, and the current audio chapter in the listening view.
  • After Save for offline reported readiness, the preview server was stopped. The reader reopened from its saved bundle. Cached audio played naturally from 30.65 seconds to 83.04 seconds across the 37.51-second chapter boundary; no server was running.
  • At 320px width, the session, listening view and reading page had no horizontal overflow; reading was checked at 28px in day and night themes.

Automated regression checks

Eleven tests cover whole-hadith planning, saved-session migration and validation, finite sessions when the collection grows, explicit advancement including skipped entries, preservation of existing preferences, media byte ranges, interrupted/invalid downloads, source-script equality and every shipped release hash. The content checker separately verifies the complete source record and Arabic hashes.

Limits and next device check

The Cedar recordings, source wording and artwork are unchanged. Independent scholarly translation review and a complete spoken-word/name audit remain outstanding. This is still an evaluation sample.

The user's previous iPhone 17 / iOS 26 Home Screen test confirmed both entries played while offline with the screen locked. The desktop checks above do not certify that behavior after this shell update. Repeat the saved Home Screen session, locked playback, boundary, pause/resume and return-to-reading flow on the iPhone.

This is not a full VoiceOver or accessibility audit. Theme colours and the 21px default remain unchanged. No 208-app research claim is implied: the earlier study records 208 public references, 20 closer studies and five limited browser interaction samples.
