Working recommendation

B gives the app its purpose. A keeps the reading quiet. C gives listening room.

Use a small session as the entry model, with direct resume for returning readers. Once reading begins, borrow A’s restrained layout. Keep C’s illustrated player as a distinct listening view. This is a design hypothesis to test with you, not a claim that three concepts should become three permanent modes.

What changes the formula

Organize around the reading,
not around a page template.

01 / Entry

Let a return be a return.

The existing reader repeats branding, book context, art, title, sources, audio and chain controls before the narration. Returning should resume the chosen hadith, while the welcome scene serves the first visit or an intentional pause.

Reference: Apple Books’ place recovery, Yomu’s reading-first defaults.

02 / The unit

One complete hadith.

The hadith is the unit of navigation, bookmarking, audio and source identity. A session is a small ordered set of those units. Never divide a narration into invented slides, excerpt cards or a timer’s arbitrary endpoint.

Reference: Greentech’s hadith navigation, Serial Reader’s bounded portions. Whole-hadith boundaries are our project constraint.

03 / Trust

Close sources. Clear status.

Arabic, the fixed digital revision, print witness and translation comparisons belong to the current hadith. Open them without losing the reading position. “Sahih al-Bukhari” and “working English translation” describe different things and must remain distinct.

Reference: Sefaria’s resource panel, Sunnah.com’s reference structure.

04 / Listening

Show what is playing and what follows.

A visible ordered queue and measured chapter boundaries make the 87-second sample understandable. Keep one persistent player across reading and sources. Use pre-generated downloads; do not silently replace reviewed recordings with live TTS.

Reference: Libby’s two levels of progress, Castro’s queue, Readwise’s documented offline TTS limit.

05 / Atmosphere

Art has a role, not a quota.

Give art room at entry, listening and the end of a session. During reading, use a still, compact horizon or a quiet margin. Do not insert an illustration between the source’s clauses or imply a scene depicts the hadith or actual Paradise.

Visual analogues: Portal’s sense of place, Emergence’s editorial environment. Placement is our design judgment, not a proven calming effect.

06 / Comfort

Keep the page adaptable.

Preserve 21px text, left alignment, sensible line lengths, day/night surfaces and larger type. Continuous scrolling avoids introducing a page-turn gesture that can conflict with selection or source panels.

Reference: Adobe’s reflow model, Readwise’s pagination tradeoff, WCAG reflow.

Patterns deliberately left out

AI summaries and interpretation • invented thematic titles • streaks and leaderboards • forced daily unlocks • decorative word highlighting without timing data • endless feeds • automatic source-text editing • unreviewed audio trimming • moving backgrounds behind narration.

Read · Listen · Sources

Views share the same selected entry. Opening a source retains context.

Keep the current content checks, persistent audio controller and verified offline bundle beneath the selected interface. The experiments reuse the canonical content and recording directly; they do not fork a second translation or replace the working service worker.

Before expanding: independent scholarly text review and exact spoken-word/name review remain outstanding. Layout research does not certify the translation. Real iPhone offline and locked-screen testing must be repeated after the chosen shell is integrated.

How we decide

Try the same small tasks.

  1. Open Hadith 1, read its body, then expand the chain.
  2. Open Arabic & sources, close it, and check whether you still know where you were.
  3. Listen, pause, open sources, and return to the same recording.
  4. Continue to Hadith 2 and finish the sample.
  5. Repeat in night mode and with the largest text size.

Judge ease of returning, source confidence, thumb reach, text comfort and whether the art helps you settle. Novelty alone is not a success criterion. The comparison control above can put all three concepts on Hadith 1.
