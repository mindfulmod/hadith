Classical discussion

Ibn Hajar identifies clause A with Karimah’s transmission from al-Kushmihani, records clause B for most transmitters, and judges the Karimah form anomalous when comparing other transmissions. He also records the explicit ten-days ending. This is his assessment of a wording variant, not a blanket rejection of the underlying hadith.

Read Fath al-Bari · pages 531–533 ↗

The app cannot make clause A mean clause B through an unlabelled English rendering. Selecting a transmission and translating it are separate decisions.
