A continued narrator chain

This entry continues the transmission sequence that begins at Hadith 935. Its own opening words are preserved; the earlier chain is available through the link below.

Read the preceding full chain · 935
Narrator chain

Ibn Jurayj said: Al-Hasan ibn Muslim also informed me, from Tawus, from Ibn ‘Abbas, may Allah Most High be pleased with them both. He said:

Report

I attended Fitr with the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and Abu Bakr, ‘Umar and ‘Uthman, may Allah Most High be pleased with them. They would pray it before the sermon, then he would deliver the sermon afterwards. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, went out. It is as though I am looking at him as he motions with his hand for people to sit. Then he went forward, making his way through them, until he came to the women with Bilal. He said, ‘O Prophet, when believing women come to you pledging allegiance to you’—the verse. When he finished it, he said, ‘Are you committed to that?’ One woman among them said, ‘Yes’; no one else answered him.

Narrator / transmission note

Hasan did not know who she was.

Report

He said, ‘Then give charity.’ Bilal spread out his garment, then said, ‘Come, may my father and mother be ransomed for you.’ They began throwing large rings and rings into Bilal’s garment.

Narrator / transmission note

Hadith 945

Dar Ibn Kathir 989 · Bewley 945 · EPUB 2.15.104

Narrator chain

Abu al-Walid related to us. He said: Shu‘bah related to us. He said: ‘Adi ibn Thabit related to me. He said: I heard Sa‘id ibn Jubayr, from Ibn ‘Abbas:

Report

The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, went out on the day of Fitr and prayed two rak‘ahs. He did not pray before it or after it, and Bilal was with him.

  • Both before-it and after-it clauses, two rak‘ahs and Bilal’s presence remain. The separate Abu al-Mu‘alla chapter report is preserved outside the numbered entry and its narration.
