The first two entries use the existing AI-assisted English working translations. Their status has not changed through this design work. They are not Darussalam’s English text and have not received independent scholarly certification. All source blocks are carried into the reader verbatim.

What we learned from the library

I inspected the MindfulMod bookshelf, the Timeless Seeds welcome screen, reading 97, and its reading settings. The useful foundation is the warm, inhabited-feeling landscape, book typography, saved place, explicit source access, and user-controlled appearance. The user specifically values feeling present in a warm tropical island environment and reads in both daylight and at night.

In the inspected Timeless Seeds reading, the original passage follows a large illustration and explanation. For this companion, the hadith becomes the primary reading surface. The welcome screen carries the large scene; the reader uses a compact image strip. The text remains on an opaque surface. Sources sit beside the hadith number. There are no new explanations or thematic paraphrases in the reading flow.

The design questions, answered

QuestionDecision and basis
What should opening the app accomplish?Return to the saved place in one tap. A calm welcome gives context; it does not require a daily choice among competing recommendations.
How much is one reading?One complete hadith, in book order. Later sessions group whole entries around a rough 5–10 minute target. These two entries contain 264 English words including chains; the UI calls them a short sample.
What should stay visible?The hadith number, narration, save control and source access. At the user’s request, the full narrator chain is expandable. It is not removed from content or future narration.
How does art help?A consistent imagined tropical veranda establishes the atmosphere. A compact crop marks entry into a reading. Art never depicts the hadith’s events or embeds religious text.
How should a session end?An explicit next-hadith action and optional “Mark as read.” No automatic completion just from scrolling, forced timer, streak or confetti.
What does trust look like?Visible “Working translation,” full Arabic, named comparison sources, pinned revision, source pages, licence and comparison notes. Authenticity of the collection must not be confused with certification of our digital transcription or translation.
What belongs in settings?Day/night/device, four font sizes, serif or sans, expanded line spacing and artwork visibility. These are meaningful reading preferences; avoid a large customization dashboard.

This palette is an art-direction choice informed by the user’s preference, not a medical claim that green relaxes everyone. Elliot’s review of colour psychology describes substantial methodological and contextual limitations. We should judge this specific pairing on the actual phone in both lighting conditions.

Dark-on-light has shown proofreading advantages, particularly at small type sizes, in Piepenbrock, Mayr & Buchner (2014). That does not establish one best theme for this reader or all lighting conditions. Provide both themes, follow the device initially, and allow a deliberate override.

The WCAG AA normal-text minimum is 4.5:1. Our secondary text is 6.05:1 by day and 7.66:1 by night; action labels are 6.47:1 and 9.51:1. Soft atmosphere does not require low-contrast words. Gold is used for ornament and accents.

Reading and visual rhythm

  • Start at 21px with 1.75 line height, adjustable to 18, 24 or 28px. Use a familiar book serif with a system sans option. These values are starting design decisions, not universal optimal measurements.
  • Constrain the desktop reading measure, left-align English, and preserve natural wrapping. Use generous paragraph separation and responsive margins.
  • Keep the source block intact. Do not turn clauses into swipe cards, insert pull quotes, add emphasis to selected religious phrases, or truncate the narration to fit a screen.
  • Let a long narration scroll naturally. Reader navigation advances by complete hadith; browser Back remains meaningful.
  • Use Arabic language and right-to-left direction explicitly. Avoid letter spacing or decorative fonts on Arabic text.

WCAG’s AAA visual-presentation guidance supports adjustable colour, non-justified text, limited line width and spacing. We use it as a design reference; this is not a claim of full AAA compliance. AA text-spacing guidance requires layouts to tolerate user spacing overrides without losing content. Reflow guidance informs the 320 CSS-pixel check.

Wallace and colleagues’ 2022 font study found considerable individual differences in reading speed across fonts. It supports trying real passages and avoiding a claim that one typeface is best for everyone. Our goal is comfortable, accurate reading, not maximum speed.

The reader uses progressive disclosure for secondary material: source detail and user-requested narrator chains. The controls remain explicitly labelled and adjacent to the relevant hadith. The chain must be available to keyboards and screen readers, not dependent on an unexplained gesture.

Art that establishes a place

The selected direction combines a warm limestone veranda, palms, flowering vines and a calm sea with restrained geometric carving. Day and night share the same scene. The large illustration belongs to the welcome screen; the reading page has an 80px strip on phones and 120px on desktop. The reading surface remains opaque. Pictures can be hidden.

Research on irrelevant decorative details gives reason to avoid assuming that more illustration improves comprehension. A 2021 experimental paper on the seductive-details effect also highlights confounds and inconsistent outcomes. Those learning experiments are not direct evidence about this spiritual reader. The practical hypothesis to test here is that atmosphere encourages return while bounded placement preserves attention.

Artwork is an imagined reading environment, not religious evidence or a historical reconstruction. Generated imagery contains no religious figures, calligraphy or fabricated quotations. The two original PNG assets are in reader/assets/; the prompt record is reader/assets/provenance.json.

Application architecture

LayerResponsibility
Source recordresearch/bukhari-opening-review.json is the authoritative reviewed input. Preserve exact Arabic and English blocks, stable entry IDs, provenance, witness differences, licences and review status. This data must not be rewritten by a UI formatter.
Content bundlereader/content.js is generated verbatim from that input. A validation script compares all blocks to the source and checks Arabic hashes. Later load versioned book bundles on demand, rather than shipping the full collection at startup.
Presentationreader/index.html, reader/reader.css and reader/reader.js own the page, themes, routing, source dialog and labelled disclosures. Editorial labels are kept outside the source blocks. Route IDs are stable.
Personal statereader/store.js stores preferences, current hadith, per-entry scroll positions, bookmarks and explicit read markers. It has its own versioned key, separate from the audio pilot. No account or cloud sync is implied. Storage failure must not prevent reading. An export/restore feature is a later durability improvement.
Audio — next stageRetain the persistent player tested in the pilot. Each future recording needs entry ID, text revision/hash, exact spoken script, honorific policy, voice/settings, file hash, duration and listening-review status. Revised text invalidates the associated approved recording until rechecked.
Offline — next stageVersion shell, text and audio independently. Download a complete session with explicit progress, verified file completion and a clear ready state. Keep incomplete downloads distinct. Handle byte ranges for audio. Never replace a session’s text or audio halfway through playback.

Keep this a small static app on the existing GitHub Pages site. The current reader is a separately previewable route under /reader/, allowing layout and content review while the existing listening pilot stays available. No backend or framework is necessary for two entries. The first reader preview does not register its own offline worker or claim to offer hadith audio.

WebKit documents best-effort storage and possible eviction, so “downloaded” must describe currently verified local files, not a permanent guarantee. Request persistent storage when integrating offline downloads, and recheck availability before playing. WebKit also describes separate Home Screen app storage; a download in a Safari tab is not proof that the installed app has the same files.

What must be tested before expanding

  1. Read both entries in day and night modes on the iPhone. Assess comfortable line tracking, perceived text density and whether the compact artwork gives enough of the island atmosphere.
  2. Find the Arabic and printed source page, return to reading, expand the chain, bookmark an entry and reopen it. No lost place or unexplained modal navigation.
  3. Check 320px reflow, 200% text enlargement, keyboard focus, native dialog dismissal, screen-reader labels, reduced motion, and contrast. Controls use at least 44px touch height, following the enhanced target-size reference where applicable.
  4. Check every English and Arabic block against the source record, including punctuation and speaker boundaries. A content hash is a change detector, not evidence of scholarly correctness.
  5. For audio, produce one connected-phrase Cedar audition using an approved complete script. Listen against the text for names, honorifics, omissions, repetitions and synthetic phrasing. Speech recognition alone cannot sign it off.
  6. For offline listening, finish the download inside the Home Screen app, enable airplane mode, lock the phone and cross every hadith boundary. Compare separate recordings with a preassembled continuous session. The previous airplane-mode report is unresolved; desktop results cannot certify iPhone background behavior.

Research distinguishes standards, empirical findings and our design hypotheses. The next useful feedback is from the actual first two readings on the user’s phone, not additional generic preference research.
