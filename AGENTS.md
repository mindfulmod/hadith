# Hadith artwork direction

For any artwork generation, image replacement or visual-world changes, read `research/visual-world.json` and inspect the relevant original reference images before starting. The illustrated guide is `research/visual-world.html`.

The user-approved anchors are 03, 07, 16, 17, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25 and 32 in `landscapes/assets/`. They establish a shared world for future imagery. They are not approval for daily rotation, per-hadith scene assignment or a landscape picker.

Keep the shared warm light, materials, painterly naturalism and welcoming atmosphere while varying the landscape and composition. Follow the region-specific rules, reference roles, image-role briefs and review questions in the guide. New images remain candidates until the user accepts them into the reference set. Preserve original assets and generation provenance.

Artwork must not modify, paraphrase, illustrate or interpret the hadith source wording. Preserve the reader's existing content-verification workflow described in `README.md`.

## Reader interface colours

The user rejected green as the primary colour on 17 October 2026. Keep the reader interface free of green-dominant backgrounds, text and controls. The current proposal is parchment/cocoa with muted rose in day mode, and warm plum with apricot in night mode; it remains open to user feedback. Natural foliage in the existing artwork is separate from the interface palette. Preserve the comfortable 21px default reading size.
